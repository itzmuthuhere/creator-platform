# Sources: Credit card EMI vs personal loan

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [SmartEMI](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/smartemi), HDFC Bank. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: from 0.99%/month; Min IRR 10.80%, Max 24.96%, Avg 18.20%, APR 21.57%; fee up to ₹999 + GST; repayment = EMI plus GST; 6–48 months; amount blocked against the credit limit; reward points reversed.
- [Personal Loan Interest Rates, Fees & Charges](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/personal-loan/interest-rates-and-charges), HDFC Bank, Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: rack 9.99–24%; Q1 FY27 IRR 9.97/24.06/avg 10.89; APR avg 11.18; fee up to ₹6,500 + GST; part-payment charges 4/3/2%.
- [Most Important Terms & Conditions, Version 4.12](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/content/dam/hdfcbankpws/in/en/personal-banking/discover-products/cards/credit-cards/personal-mitc/mitc-in-english.pdf), HDFC Bank, Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: revolving 3.75%/month (45% a year) on most variants; SmartEMI pre-closure 3% of balance principal; SmartEMI fees (₹249 pay-in-parts from 1 Oct 2026; up to ₹849); reward points reversed on SmartEMI.

- [Flexipay EMI Low ROI Terms and Conditions (15 Jun–14 Jul 2026)](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/tnc/flexipay-emi-low-roi-tnc-15june26to14Jul26.pdf), SBI Card. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: Flexipay 10–25% from 15 Jun 2026; rate tiers by bureau score; fee 1% or ₹2,000 whichever is less; limit blocked for principal + fee + GST.
