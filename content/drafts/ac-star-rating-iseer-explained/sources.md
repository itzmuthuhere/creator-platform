# Sources: AC tonnage and star ratings: what the ISEER number means

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.
Format: `- [Page title](url) — Publisher. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD. Used for: what it supported.`

## Primary / official

- [Room Air Conditioners: notifications and amendments](https://www.beestarlabel.com/Content/Files/AC_Notification.pdf) — Bureau of Energy Efficiency (Star Label portal). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: list of AC labelling notifications, including Amendment V, S.O. 3984(E) of 28 Aug 2025, and the Appliance Labeling and Compliance Regulations 2026 (w.e.f. 1 Jan 2026).

## Reputable secondary

- [What is the star rating label of an air conditioner telling you?](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/energy/what-is-the-star-rating-label-of-an-air-conditioner-telling-you) — Down To Earth (Sugeet Grover), 22 Sep 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: ISEER definition and 2015 introduction; 24–43°C bins from 54 cities; 1,600-hour basis and its limits; regional differences; average 3-star and 5-star annual units.
- [BEE Star Rating Validity Extension for Room AC](https://www.bl-india.com/notifications/bee-registration/instructions-for-room-air-conditioners) — Brand Liaison (summary of BEE notice dated 1 Aug 2024). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: previous star table extended to 31 Dec 2025; label valid 1 Jul 2022 to 31 Dec 2025.

## Source videos

- [Which AC Should You Buy In 2026 | 3 Star VS 5 Star](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnXo9f-5nu8) — ClearBuy (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: checking ISEER within a star band; cooling capacity in watts vs tonnage; price gap estimate (attributed).
- [BEE New Star Rating 2026: Full Guide for AC Buyers in India](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YILZsm_A7V0) — Electro by Keshav (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: old-label stock vs new 2026 labels in shops.
- [What is ISEER? Difference between EER & ISEER](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCxHyUTD_Hc) — Girish Shinde (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: EER (single condition) vs ISEER (seasonal).
