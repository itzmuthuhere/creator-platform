# Sources: How to report online fraud in India (1930 and cybercrime.gov.in)

One line per source actually consulted. Only pages opened and read are listed.

## Primary / official

- [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) — Ministry of Home Affairs / I4C. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: complaint categories, Suspect Repository and Report Suspect facilities, alert on fake mails.
- [NCRP Money Restoration Portal: How to apply](https://mrm-ncrp.mha.gov.in/public-info) — I4C, MHA. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: refund request steps (14-digit acknowledgement ID, OTP, refund account, PAN, court order upload, MR request ID).
- [Limiting Liability of Customers in Unauthorised Electronic Banking Transactions (6 July 2017)](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/english/scripts/Notification.aspx?Id=2336) — RBI. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: current liability rules (3 / 4–7 / beyond 7 working days, caps, 10-day credit, 90-day resolution, burden of proof).
- [Draft amendment directions press release (6 March 2026)](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=62340) — RBI. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: the direction of the revision (broader scope, faster processing, compensation for small-value frauds).
- [Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026: FAQ](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/english/scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=3407) — RBI. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: effective 1 July 2026, 30-day and 90-day rules, cms.rbi.org.in, 14448, free.

## Reputable secondary

- [RBI caps customer liability in digital fraud from 2027](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-rbi-final-amendment-directions-limiting-customer-liability-digital-transactions-effect-2027/) — MediaNama, June 2026. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: final directions, effective 1 Jan 2027, 5-day zero-liability window, 45/60-day resolution, compensation terms.
- [MHA's new SOP on cyber financial frauds](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/mhas-new-sop-on-cyber-financial-frauds) — Drishti IAS. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: SOP (Jan 2026), refunds below ₹50,000 without a court order, 90-day freeze lifting, CFCFRMS with 85+ banks.
- [1 lakh citizens benefit from cyber fraud refund system](https://www.moneylife.in/article/1-lakh-citizens-benefit-from-cyber-fraud-refund-system-as-mha-moves-to-speed-up-restoration-of-lost-money/80811.html) — Moneylife. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: MRM portal address and figures.
