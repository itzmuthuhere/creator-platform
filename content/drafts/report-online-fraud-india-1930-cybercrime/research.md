# Research notes: How to report online fraud in India (1930 and cybercrime.gov.in)

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Kaustav Chowdhury, "How to File a Cybercrime Complaint Online in India?"** (4:05, English): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HMHeP5QWFFg. Call 1930 immediately (golden hour), the portal's categories, evidence to collect, acknowledgement number, IT Act/BNS sections.
- **CopShashank17** (3:48, Hindi): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IXssQBjAXVs. A police officer: keep calling 1930 from other phones if it's busy; otherwise the portal; screenshot and UTR; the first hour matters because money moves account to account; after the freeze, get a court order through a lawyer.
- **Cyber Helper** (10:37, Hindi): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5XBi_B5kfAI. Detailed portal walkthrough: Financial Fraud → Register a complaint → accept → state, email → incident details (UPI category, "have you lost money"), bank/wallet, UTR (12 digits), amount, date/time, suspect details; warns that mixing up victim and accused fields can get your own account blocked; the complaint goes to nodal officers who put holds down the chain.
- **Online Legal Center (Adv. Ayush Garg)** (4:47, Hindi): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=okmUYP92Sqk. The complaint only freezes money; releasing it needs a magistrate's order (old CrPC 451/457, now BNSS 497/503 per the video), proving the transaction is genuine; High Court writ if stuck.
- **Aaj Tak** (4:47, Hindi): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HQlmnRX64rQ. RBI guideline: report to the bank within 3 days; be truthful with the bank even if you shared an OTP; prevention tips.

## 2. Topic assessment

**Verdict:** PROCEED. Very high value. The rules changed in 2026 (MHA SOP Jan 2026; Money Restoration Module; RBI final liability directions June 2026 effective 1 Jan 2027; RB-IOS 2026 from 1 July 2026), and none of the videos cover these.

**Angle:** two parallel tracks, freezing the money (1930/NCRP) and bank liability, plus the new ways to get frozen money back, with the dates that matter.
