- [FAQ/HELP](https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in/Home/FAQ), Bima Bharosa (IRDAI). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: insurer to resolve within 15 days; complaint status flow (escalated, reopened); token tracking.
