# Sources: ITR-U updated return

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [Income Tax Returns FAQs](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/%20income%20tax%20returns-faq), Income Tax Department e-filing portal, last updated 3 Oct 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: Section 263(6) of the 2025 Act and Section 139(8A) of the old Act; the key ITR-U conditions (filed or not, no lower tax/higher refund/enhanced loss, one per year, additional tax under s.267); 48 months; AY 2026-27 ITR-U under the old Act; belated return for AY 2026-27 by 31 Dec 2026 with the ₹1,000/₹5,000 fee; earlier years' belated/revised closed, ITR-U still open.

## Reputable secondary

- [Updated Return (ITR-U) Rules 2026: Who Can File](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/updated-return-itr-u-rules-2026-who-can-file-procedure-section-263), Patron Accounting, 23 Mar 2026 (updated 6 Apr 2026). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the 25/50/60/70% table; the search/survey bar; Budget 2026 changes (after reassessment +10%, loss reduction); pay before filing.
- [Budget 2026 Relief: Updated ITR Filing Allowed After Reassessment Notice](https://blog.saginfotech.com/budget-2026-updated-itr-filing-allowed-reassessment-notice-it-act-2025), SAG Infotech, 4 Feb 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: ITR-U in response to a reassessment notice within the time the notice allows; +10%; penalty exclusion.
- [CBDT Issues Fresh Guidelines For Condonation Of Delay Under Section 119(2)(b)](https://www.mondaq.com/india/tax-authorities/1543964/cbdt-issues-fresh-guidelines-for-handling-applications-for-condonation-of-delay-under-section-1192b-for-refunds-and-loss-claims), Mondaq. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: Circular 11/2024 of 1 Oct 2024; the five-year limit; monetary limits; reasonable cause; no interest for the delay.
