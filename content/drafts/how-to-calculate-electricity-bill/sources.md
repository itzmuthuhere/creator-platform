One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [Know your Star Label & Make a Right Choice, Session 2](https://www.beestarlabel.com/Content/Files/Session_2.pdf) — Bureau of Energy Efficiency. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: refrigerator star label parameter is annual energy consumption (lower is better); checking labels on beestarlabel.com.

## Reputable secondary

- [Free power supply up to 200 units to benefit 1.46 cr domestic consumers](https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/free-power-supply-up-to-200-units-to-benefit-146-cr-domestic-consumers) — DT Next, 10 May 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 200 free units for ≤500-unit bimonthly users; consumer counts by band; ₹2.35/unit subsidy on 101–200; >500 users no benefit; subsidy cost.
- [TNEB/TNPDCL Tariff July 2026 — Free 200 Units, Rates & Pending Revision](https://thediscombill.com/guides/tamil-nadu-electricity-tariff-july-2026/) — TheDiscomBill, updated 24 Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: G.O. (Ms) No. 50 of 10 May 2026 and TNERC Order No. 5 of 2026; no fixed charge; bimonthly billing; pending inflation-linked revision.
- [TNEB Old vs New Tariff 2026: 100 vs 200 Free Units](https://electricitybillcalc.com/guides/tneb-old-vs-new-tariff-2026/) — ElectricityBillCalc, Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: consumer rates ₹4.70 (201–400) and ₹6.30 (401–500) for ≤500 units. Its >500 table conflicts with DT Next on the 101–200 slab and wasn't followed.
