## Primary / official

- [Find, secure, or erase a lost Android device](https://support.google.com/android/answer/6160491?hl=en) — Android Help (Google). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: requirements; play sound, mark as lost, factory reset; android.com/find and guest mode; crowdsourced network; accuracy ranges; Google password after erase.
- [Sanchar Saathi](https://www.sancharsaathi.gov.in/) — Department of Telecommunications. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: block/unblock/status services for lost or stolen handsets; counts of phones blocked and traced.
- [CEIR request for blocking lost/stolen mobile](https://www.ceir.gov.in/Request/CeirUserBlockRequestDirect.jsp) — DoT / C-DOT. Accessed 2026-10-03 (form viewed, not submitted). Used for: prerequisites (police report, re-issued SIM for OTP, ID proof, optional invoice, IMEI) and the details the form asks for.
