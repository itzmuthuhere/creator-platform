## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** How to Find Your Lost Android Phone with Google Find Hub (Smartphones Made Easy), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U_Yq_8ionKI. 12 days old. Prep: screen lock, location on, Google account signed in (remember password), Find Hub on; when lost: android.com/find (or search), sign in, see map/last known location, play sound, mark as lost with message and number (no sensitive info), mark found; notes on remote reset.
- **Second:** Find My Device (Google Find Hub): 5 settings (Mufungo Geeks Tech), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WVW8psrzBeI. ~1 month old. Allow device to be located; Find your offline devices (off / without network / busy places / everywhere); improve location accuracy; Remote Lock via android.com/lock with phone number (device online; twice a day); theft detection lock, offline device lock.
- **Third:** மொபைல் தொலைந்து போச்சா? FIR | CEIR (Our Tech Tamil), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cWJh6w9JS1s. ~1 year old, Tamil; captions unusable. Police complaint + CEIR blocking.
