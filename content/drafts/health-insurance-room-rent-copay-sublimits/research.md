## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** AVOID This HIDDEN Trap | Room Rent Limit in Health Insurance (Ditto Insurance), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lb47l_npCqY. 4:02, ~1 year old.
- **Second:** How I lost money due to room rent sub limit clause in health insurance (freefincal), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2o1qqemylDU. 6:40, ~7 years old (claim from Dec 2018).
- **Third:** Room Rent Limit In Health Insurance (Niva Bupa), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P35DgFxFiAI. 9:29, ~10 months old, Hindi.
- **Fourth:** Co-Payment in Health Insurance EXPLAINED (Gurleen Kaur Tikku), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6NSllnpDU8E. 8:51, ~3 years old, Hindi.

**Ditto:** room-rent limit types (fixed, % of SI, room category); choosing a costlier room triggers proportionate deduction "of the entire bill" (₹5,000 limit, ₹10,000 room, ₹2 lakh bill → ₹1 lakh paid); ICU usually separate; newer plans have no room limit.

**freefincal:** his mother's 2018 reimbursement claim on a ₹5.5 lakh PSU policy: room ₹5,500 vs an ailment-specific cap of ₹5,000 applied by the TPA; surgeon, consultation and OT fees cut proportionately, medicines paid in full; ~₹24,000 lost; older insurers may cap sum insured increases at old age.

**Niva Bupa:** financial vs category limits; 1% of SI for small policies; proportionate deduction across surgery and doctor fees; worked example.

**Gurleen:** co-pay as a share of every claim; mandatory co-pay (often senior-citizen plans or after 60/61), voluntary co-pay for lower premium, zone-based co-pay; check the policy.

Useful material:
- Types of room-rent limits; proportionate deduction mechanism.
- A real settlement showing which items were cut (freefincal).
- Co-pay types.
