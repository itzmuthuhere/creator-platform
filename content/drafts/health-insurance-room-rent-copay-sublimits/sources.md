## Primary / official

- [Modified Guidelines on Product filing in Health Insurance Business – Norms on Proportionate Deductions](https://irdai.gov.in/documents/37343/365525/Modified+Guidelines+on+Product+filing+in+Health+Insurance+Business-Norms+on.pdf/7b50effc-e553-f0a6-389d-3ca90ab11af5?version=1.0&t=1631531502137&download=true) — IRDAI, 11 Jun 2020 (IRDAI/HLT/REG/CIR/151/06/2020). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: proportionate deduction only on defined "associated medical expenses"; pharmacy and consumables, implants and medical devices, diagnostics excluded; none at hospitals without differential billing; none on ICU charges; applies to products filed from 1 Oct 2020 and existing policies on renewal from 1 Apr 2021.
- [FAQs on Standard Health Product Arogya Sanjeevani](https://irdai.gov.in/faq-s-on-standard-health-product-arogya-sanjeevani-product) — IRDAI. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: standard limits (room 2% of SI max ₹5,000/day, ICU 5% max ₹10,000, 5% co-pay, cataract 25% or ₹40,000, modern treatments 50%).
- [Master Circular on Health Insurance Business, 2024](https://irdai.gov.in/documents/37343/365525/%e0%a4%b8%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%b5%e0%a4%be%e0%a4%b8%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%a5%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%af+%e0%a4%ac%e0%a5%80%e0%a4%ae%e0%a4%be+%e0%a4%b5%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%af%e0%a4%b5%e0%a4%b8%e0%a4%be%e0%a4%af+%e0%a4%aa%e0%a4%b0+%e0%a4%ae%e0%a4%be%e0%a4%b8%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%9f%e0%a4%b0+%e0%a4%aa%e0%a4%b0%e0%a4%bf%e0%a4%aa%e0%a4%a4%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%b0+_+Master+Circular++on+Health++Insurance+Business++29052024.pdf/5e707a91-b5de-1ec1-cf18-b66273a6839d?t=1716962621002&version=1.0) — IRDAI, 29 May 2024. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: the Customer Information Sheet must show sub-limits, deductibles and waiting periods.
- [Arogya Sanjeevani Policy](https://www.newindia.co.in/health-insurance/arogya-sanjeevani-policy) — New India Assurance (UIN NIAHLIP25044V022425). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: current terms and sum insured range ₹50,000–₹10 lakh.
- [Arogya Sanjeevani Policy wording](https://www.eindiainsurance.com/brochure/cholamandalam-health-arogya-sanjeevani-policy-wording.pdf) — Cholamandalam MS General Insurance (hosted copy). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: loss-sharing table (room/ICU limits with proportionate deduction, cataract and modern-treatment sub-limits, 5% co-pay on admissible amount); definitions of co-payment (doesn't reduce SI), room rent (includes associated medical expenses) and sub-limit.
