# How to update your address in Aadhaar online in 2026

You can change the address on your Aadhaar online for ₹75, without visiting a centre, in one of two ways: upload a document that shows your new address, or use the Aadhaar of a family member you live with. The second route needs no address proof of your own, which helps if you've just married, moved in with family, or rent without bills in your name. Both need access to the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar, because every step uses an OTP.

This guide covers the current fees, both routes step by step, which documents UIDAI accepts, and the mistakes that get requests rejected.

## What it costs, and the "free update" confusion

UIDAI's [charges sheet](https://backend.uidai.gov.in/get/files/media/document/2026-09/Aadhaar_Enrolment__and__Update__-__English.pdf), updated in September 2026, lists:

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Address change (a "demographic update"), online or at a centre | ₹75 |
| **Document update** on the myAadhaar portal | Free until 14 June 2027 |
| Document update at an Aadhaar centre | ₹75 |
| Biometric update (photo, fingerprints, iris) | ₹125; free at ages 5–7 and 15–17, and free from 7 to 15 until 30 September 2027 |
| Email update through the Aadhaar app | Free until 31 December 2026 |

The free service is the one people mix up. A **document update** means uploading fresh proof of identity and address to support the details already on your Aadhaar, for example if your Aadhaar is ten years old and you've never updated your documents. It doesn't change your address. If your address has changed, it's an address update, and it costs ₹75.

## Route 1: with your own address proof

Use this if you have a document in your own name showing the new address.

1. Go to the myAadhaar portal from [UIDAI's My Aadhaar page](https://uidai.gov.in/en/my-aadhaar) and log in with your Aadhaar number and the OTP sent to your linked mobile.
2. Choose **Address update**, then the option to update using your documents.
3. Enter the new address exactly as it appears on your proof document. Fill each field (house number, street, locality, PIN code) carefully; this is what will print on your Aadhaar.
4. Select the document type and upload a clear scan or photo of it.
5. Pay ₹75 online.
6. Note the **Service Request Number (SRN)**. You'll use it to track the request.

## Route 2: with a family member's Aadhaar

Use this when the address proof is in someone else's name: a spouse, a parent, or another relative you live with. UIDAI calls this the **Head of Family (HoF)** route. When it was introduced, UIDAI said [any resident over 18 can be the HoF](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/nation/now-aadhaar-holders-can-update-addresses-online-with-the-consent-of-head-of-family-467049/amp) and share their Aadhaar address with relatives.

1. Log in to myAadhaar with your own Aadhaar and OTP, and choose the option to update your address using the Head of Family's details.
2. Enter the HoF's Aadhaar number. Their address will be used for yours.
3. Upload a **proof of relationship** that shows both your names and how you're related. UIDAI accepts documents such as a ration card, a mark sheet, a marriage certificate or a passport. If you have none, the HoF can sign a self-declaration in UIDAI's format instead.
4. Pay ₹75 and note the SRN.
5. The HoF gets an SMS and must log in to myAadhaar and **approve the request with their own OTP within 30 days**.

The catch: if the HoF rejects the request or doesn't approve it within 30 days, the request closes and **the ₹75 isn't refunded**. Tell the family member before you apply, and make sure they can receive OTPs on their linked mobile.

## Using the Aadhaar app

UIDAI's Aadhaar app offers address update alongside mobile number update, e-Aadhaar download, biometric lock and request tracking. You register by entering your Aadhaar number, completing face authentication on the phone and setting a six-digit PIN. The update steps are the same as on the portal: choose documents or a family member, upload, authenticate and pay. Boss Wallah's [app walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qedcY1Oh2Po) shows the screens.

The app's main extra is for people whose mobile number has changed: it lets you update the mobile number using face authentication, which you'll need before you can do anything else online.

## Which documents work as address proof

UIDAI's [list of acceptable documents](https://backend.uidai.gov.in/get/files/media/document/2026-05/List_of_Supporting_Document_for_Aadhaar_Enrolment_and_Update_0.pdf) is long. The ones most people have:

| Document | Condition |
|---|---|
| Indian passport | Valid |
| Ration card or e-ration card | With photograph |
| Voter ID | Details shown on the Election Commission website |
| Driving licence | Valid |
| Electricity, water, gas, landline, postpaid mobile or broadband bill | Not older than 3 months |
| Rent, lease or leave-and-licence agreement | Registered or unregistered |
| Registered sale or gift deed | Registered with the Registrar |
| Government accommodation allotment letter | Not older than 1 year |

Three conditions apply to every document: it must be currently valid, issued in your name, and verifiable from its source. The **3-month limit on bills** catches many people: an old electricity bill won't do.

## After you apply

Track the request on myAadhaar or the app with your SRN. Once the update is approved, download the updated e-Aadhaar from the portal for free; you can use it in place of the printed card. If you want a plastic card, you can order an Aadhaar PVC card for ₹75.

Then update the places that rely on your Aadhaar address: your bank (for KYC), your mobile operator, and any government records. If you also need a fresh PAN card after an address change, our guide to [getting a duplicate PAN card online](https://techpulzo.in/how-to-get-a-duplicate-pan-card-online) covers it.

## Common reasons for rejection

- **The address you typed doesn't match the document.** Copy it as it appears, including the house number and PIN code.
- **The document is in someone else's name.** Use the HoF route instead.
- **The bill is more than three months old.**
- **The scan is unreadable,** cropped, or missing the page with your name.
- **The relationship proof doesn't name both people** (HoF route).

If a request is rejected, you'll need to apply again and pay again, so check these before you submit.

One warning: the whole update happens on UIDAI's portal or app, so you never need to share an OTP with anyone. If someone calls offering to "update your Aadhaar address" and asks for an OTP, it's a scam; our guide to [how UPI fraud happens](https://techpulzo.in/how-upi-fraud-actually-happens-the-five-warning-signs) explains the patterns.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Aadhaar address update free in 2026?

No. Changing your address costs ₹75, online or at a centre. What's free until 14 June 2027 is a "document update" on the myAadhaar portal, which refreshes the proof documents behind your existing details but doesn't change them.

### Can I update my Aadhaar address without address proof?

Yes, if you live with a family member who has the address on their Aadhaar. Use the Head of Family option, upload a proof of relationship such as a marriage certificate or ration card, and the family member approves the request with their OTP within 30 days.

### What happens if the Head of Family doesn't approve the request?

The request closes after 30 days, or immediately if they reject it, and the ₹75 fee isn't refunded. Make sure the family member knows to expect the SMS and can log in with their own OTP.

### Is a rent agreement valid proof of address for Aadhaar?

Yes. UIDAI accepts registered or unregistered rent, lease, and leave-and-licence agreements. Utility bills are accepted too, but only if they're not older than three months.

### Do I need my registered mobile number to update my address online?

Yes. Every online update uses an OTP sent to the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar. If that number has changed, update it first, either through the Aadhaar app with face authentication or at an Aadhaar centre.

## Before you start

Check that you can receive OTPs on your Aadhaar-linked number, choose your route (your own document, or a family member's Aadhaar), and keep the proof ready as a clear scan. With those in hand, the online request takes a few minutes.
