## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** Wife के Aadhaar Card में Address Change करें बिना Address Proof | ₹75 (Smart Shop), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dMgLZXo3jms. 4 days old, Hindi. Uses the new portal myaadhaarbeta.uidai.gov.in → "I am newly married" → address update with family member's (husband's) Aadhaar via OTP; ₹75; no address proof needed (relationship-based).
- **Second:** Aadhaar Address Update in Just 5 Minutes – No Documents Needed! (Rajayugitha), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rzzYnY-O8Sg. ~4 months old, Tamil auto captions. Head-of-family style update without own address proof.
- **Third:** New Aadhaar App Explained in 2026 (Boss Wallah Daily Tamil), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qedcY1Oh2Po. ~7 months old; captions sparse. Aadhaar app: register with face authentication; mobile update with OTP + face auth + payment; address update with documents or family member; track request; refunds.
