# Sources: How to check a used phone's IMEI before buying it

One line per source actually consulted. Only pages opened and read are listed.

## Primary / official

- [CEIR home: Know Genuineness of Your Mobile Handset](https://www.ceir.gov.in/Home/index.jsp) — DoT / C-DOT. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: KYM via SMS to 14422 and web portal, *#06#, box/invoice, "avoid buying" if black-listed, duplicate or already in use.
- [CEIR IMEI Verification](https://www.ceir.gov.in/Device/CeirImeiVerification.jsp) — DoT / C-DOT. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: captcha + OTP flow before entering the IMEI.
- [Sanchar Saathi FAQ](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/Home/index.jsp) — DoT. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: KYM steps and app, what verification shows (manufacturer, type, brand, model), dual-SIM phones have two IMEIs, invalid/all-zero IMEIs not allowed, GSMA allocation.
- [If you want to buy a preowned iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/104999) — Apple. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: "iPhone Locked to Owner", don't buy with Activation Lock on, "Hello" screen means erased.
- [Help prevent others from using your device without permission (Factory Reset Protection)](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9459346?hl=en) — Google. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: FRP needs a previously synced Google Account after reset; remove your account before selling.
- [Section 42, Telecommunications Act, 2023](https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/telecommunications/42.php) — bare act text. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: s.42(3)(c) tampering, s.42(3)(f) wilful possession of equipment with tampered identifiers; up to 3 years / ₹50 lakh / both.
- [BNS Section 317: Stolen property](https://devgan.in/bns/section/317/) — bare act text. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: s.317(2) receiving or retaining stolen property, "knowing or having reason to believe"; up to 3 years, fine, or both.

- [चोरी का Mobile Repair किया तो Technician पर भी Case?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dCiPijT4t9E) — Repair My Mobile (YouTube). Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: the repair-shop guideline and SIM-owner notice accounts (attributed, unverified).
- [MUST DO Checklist Before Buying Used Phones!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2bLvpsaB3r0) — Serg Tech (YouTube). Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: physical, function and battery checks; warranty check by serial/IMEI. US carrier and finance checks not used.
- [Used Phones Buying Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nlFErav8_n8) — Trakin Tech Tamil (YouTube). Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: nothing specific (captions too fragmentary).
