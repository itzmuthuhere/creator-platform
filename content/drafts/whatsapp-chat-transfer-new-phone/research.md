# Research notes: How to move WhatsApp chats to a new phone

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** How to transfer WhatsApp chats from old phone to new phone (Hindi Android Tips), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLvPI_qAjbA. ~3 months old, Hindi. Android→Android with the built-in transfer; update WhatsApp on both; take a Google backup first as a safety net; Settings > Chats > Transfer chats; QR from new phone.
- **Second:** How To Transfer WhatsApp Chats From iPhone To Android (2026) (Techno Window), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xbHun6ljDW4. ~6 months old, English. In-app: iPhone WhatsApp > Settings > Chats > Transfer chat history > Transfer to Android; install WhatsApp on Android, register with the same number (verify via SMS to the iPhone if SIM still there); scan Android's QR with the iPhone; transfers chats, media, call history, communities, channel updates, settings.
- **Third:** How to Transfer WhatsApp Data From Android to iPhone (2026) (Shubham Kanda), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rOmVz5US0Jg. ~1 month old, Hindi. Move to iOS walkthrough; iPhone must be new/reset.
