# WhatsApp chat transfer to a new phone: the right method for Android and iPhone

You don't need a paid app to move WhatsApp to a new phone. WhatsApp and the phone makers now have free, built-in ways to do it for every combination: Android to Android, iPhone to iPhone, Android to iPhone, and iPhone to Android. The trick is picking the right one, because each direction works differently and a couple have conditions that catch people out, such as needing a freshly reset iPhone.

This guide covers the WhatsApp chat transfer to a new phone for each case, what to do before you start, and what to check if it goes wrong.

## Which method to use

| Old phone → new phone | Method | Main condition |
|---|---|---|
| Android → Android | WhatsApp's built-in chat transfer (QR code) | Both phones on the same Wi-Fi |
| iPhone → iPhone | WhatsApp's built-in chat transfer (QR code), or iCloud backup | Both phones on the same Wi-Fi |
| Android → iPhone | Apple's **Move to iOS** app | iPhone must be new or factory reset |
| iPhone → Android | WhatsApp's transfer option, or the copy step during Android setup | Same phone number; a cable for the setup route |

Whichever you use, register WhatsApp on the new phone with the **same phone number**.

## Before any transfer

- **Update WhatsApp** on both phones.
- **Charge both phones** or keep them plugged in.
- **Take a backup** on the old phone (Google account on Android, iCloud on iPhone). Hindi Android Tips' [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YLvPI_qAjbA) suggests this as a safety net, which is sensible: if the transfer fails partway, you can still restore.
- **Keep the old phone as it is** until you've checked everything arrived. Don't reset it or delete WhatsApp until then.
- **Know where your SIM is.** You'll need to receive WhatsApp's verification code on the new phone or wherever the SIM is.

## Android to Android, or iPhone to iPhone

Since 2023, WhatsApp can move your full history between two phones of the same type directly over your Wi-Fi, using a QR code. [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/30/whatsapp-introduces-new-qr-code-based-local-data-transfer-method/) that the data is encrypted and goes only between the two devices on your local network, not through the cloud.

1. Connect both phones to the **same Wi-Fi network**.
2. On the **old phone**, open WhatsApp > **Settings > Chats > Transfer chats** (the exact label varies slightly by version) and start the transfer.
3. Install WhatsApp on the **new phone**, register with the same number, and follow the prompt to transfer from your old phone.
4. Scan the QR code shown on one phone with the other, as prompted.
5. Keep both phones close and on Wi-Fi until it finishes.

For iPhone to iPhone, restoring from an iCloud backup is the other option, but the direct transfer is quicker and doesn't depend on your iCloud space.

## Android to iPhone

WhatsApp's [help page](https://faq.whatsapp.com/686469079565350/) says to use Apple's **Move to iOS** app. You'll need:

- Android 5 or later, and iOS 15.5 or later on the iPhone.
- The **Move to iOS** app on the Android phone (other transfer apps aren't supported).
- An iPhone that is **new or factory reset**, because Move to iOS only runs during iPhone setup.
- The same phone number, both phones on power, and both on the same Wi-Fi (or the Android connected to the iPhone's hotspot).

The steps:

1. Start setting up the iPhone. When it asks about transferring data, choose to move it from Android.
2. Open **Move to iOS** on the Android phone and enter the code the iPhone shows.
3. Select **WhatsApp** in the list of data to transfer. On the Android phone, tap **Start** and wait while WhatsApp prepares the data; you'll be signed out on Android.
4. Return to Move to iOS and let the transfer finish.
5. Install WhatsApp on the iPhone, log in with the same number, tap **Start** when asked, and your chats appear.

**What doesn't move:** WhatsApp says call history, your display name and media received in Channels can't be transferred. Your chats, groups, media and settings do. The Android phone keeps its copy until you delete WhatsApp or reset it.

If your iPhone is already set up, you'd have to reset it to use this route, so do the WhatsApp move as part of setting up the new iPhone.

## iPhone to Android

There are two routes.

**From inside WhatsApp.** Techno Window's [walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xbHun6ljDW4) shows the newer in-app option: on the iPhone, go to WhatsApp **Settings > Chats > Transfer chat history > Transfer to Android**. Install WhatsApp on the Android phone, register with the same number, and scan the QR code the Android phone shows using the iPhone. It moves chats, media, call history, communities and settings.

**During Android setup.** [Google's Android Help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/14669513?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS) describes copying WhatsApp along with your other data when you set up a new Android phone:

- You need a Google account, the **same phone number**, and your iPhone's **charging cable** to connect the phones.
- Choose to copy data from an iPhone during setup and follow the prompts, including scanning a QR code with the iPhone camera.
- If you skipped it, Google says you can still do it for a few days after setup through the phone's switching tool (Android Switch, or Smart Switch on Samsung).
- **Don't open WhatsApp on the new Android phone until the transfer finishes**, or you can lose messages.

## Backups as a safety net, and the storage catch

A backup isn't the same as a transfer, but it's your fallback if something goes wrong.

- **Android** backs up to your Google account. Since the first half of 2024, those backups [count towards your Google storage](https://9to5google.com/2023/11/14/whatsapp-backup-google-account-storage/), which is 15 GB free across Gmail, Drive and Photos. A large backup with videos can fill it. Excluding videos from the backup or clearing large media first helps.
- **iPhone** backs up to iCloud, which uses your iCloud storage.

If your cloud space is tight, compare the options in our guide to [Google Drive, OneDrive and iCloud](https://techpulzo.in/google-drive-vs-onedrive-vs-icloud-which-cloud-storage-to-use). WhatsApp also offers end-to-end encrypted backups, protected by a password or key you set; our explainer on [what end-to-end encryption means](https://techpulzo.in/what-end-to-end-encrypted-actually-means-for-an-app-you-use-daily) covers why that matters.

## Troubleshooting

- **The transfer option doesn't appear:** update WhatsApp on both phones and check you're using the same number.
- **The phones can't find each other:** put both on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network that isolates devices), and keep Bluetooth and location on if asked.
- **Move to iOS doesn't show WhatsApp:** the iPhone must be in setup mode, which means new or reset, and WhatsApp on Android must be up to date.
- **Media is missing afterwards:** large transfers can take a while to finish in the background; leave the new phone on Wi-Fi and charging for some time before assuming something's lost.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I transfer WhatsApp chats from my old Android phone to a new one?

Put both phones on the same Wi-Fi network. On the old phone, open WhatsApp's chat settings and choose the option to transfer chats. Install WhatsApp on the new phone with the same number and scan the QR code when prompted. The transfer happens directly between the phones over your network.

### Can I move WhatsApp from Android to an iPhone that's already set up?

Not with the official method. Move to iOS only works while setting up an iPhone, so the iPhone has to be new or factory reset. If you've already set it up, you'd need to reset it and do the transfer during setup.

### Does WhatsApp chat transfer need internet?

The same-platform transfer needs both phones on the same Wi-Fi network; WhatsApp says the data goes directly between them over that local network. You'll still need a connection to download and register WhatsApp on the new phone.

### Will I lose my call history when switching from Android to iPhone?

Yes. WhatsApp says call history, your display name and media received in Channels can't be transferred from Android to iPhone. Chats, groups, media and settings do transfer.

### Do I need a third-party app to transfer WhatsApp between Android and iPhone?

No. Apple's Move to iOS handles Android to iPhone, and WhatsApp's own transfer option or Android's setup copy step handles iPhone to Android. Paid third-party tools aren't needed for a normal switch.
