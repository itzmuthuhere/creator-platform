# Sources: Emergency fund, how much and where to keep it

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.
Format: `- [Page title](url) — Publisher. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD. Used for: what it supported.`

## Primary / official

- [Multi Option Deposit Scheme (MODS)](https://sbi.bank.in/web/personal-banking/investments-deposits/deposits/mod) — SBI, updated 1 Dec 2025. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: auto-sweep from savings above ₹50,000 into term deposits in ₹5,000 units; reverse sweep in ₹5,000 units; only the broken part loses interest; 1–5 year tenor.
- [Retail Domestic Term Deposits (Below Rs. 3 crore)](https://sbi.bank.in/web/interest-rates/deposit-rates/retail-domestic-term-deposits) — SBI, updated 16 Jun 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: FD rates w.e.f. 15 Dec 2025 (46–179 days 4.90%, 1–2 years 6.25%); screenshot.
- [Savings Bank Deposits – Interest Rates](https://sbi.bank.in/web/interest-rates/savings-bank-deposits) — SBI. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: 2.50% on all balances w.e.f. 15 Jun 2025.
- [Deposit Rates](https://sbi.bank.in/web/interest-rates/interest-rates/deposit-rates) — SBI. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: premature withdrawal penalty of 0.50% (up to ₹5 lakh) and 1% (above), no interest under 7 days.
- [FAQs](https://www.dicgc.org.in/FAQs) — DICGC. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: ₹5 lakh cover per depositor per bank including interest, branches aggregated, separate cover per bank, payment within 90 days under all-inclusive directions.
- [Instant Access Facility](https://amc.ppfas.com/instant_access_facility/) — PPFAS Mutual Fund. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: ₹50,000 or 90% of holding, per investor per day per scheme at PAN level; resident individuals with non-demat holdings; IMPS within 30 minutes, 24×7.
- [NAVAll.txt](https://portal.amfiindia.com/spages/NAVAll.txt) and [NAV history report](https://portal.amfiindia.com/DownloadNAVHistoryReport_Po.aspx?tp=1&frmdt=01-Oct-2025&todt=03-Oct-2025) — AMFI. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: one-year returns of seven large liquid funds (direct growth), 6.46–6.57%.
- [Taxation of Mutual Funds](https://www.tatamutualfund.com/blogs/taxation-mutual-funds-what-every-investor-needs-know-stcg-ltcg-dividend-income-and-elss) — Tata Mutual Fund. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: debt fund gains on units bought after 31 Mar 2023 taxed at slab rates.

## Reputable secondary

- [Liquid Fund Instant Redemption: Limits You Should Know](https://www.valueresearchonline.com/learn/debt-funds/liquid-fund-instant-redemption-rs-50000-limit/) — Value Research, updated 27 Jan 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: SEBI's ₹50,000/90% per-day cap; normal one-working-day settlement; liquid funds hold instruments maturing within 91 days; instant redemption isn't a full emergency plan.
- [SEBI allows graded exit load in liquid funds](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/17548-sebi-allows-graded-exit-load-in-liquid-funds) — Cafemutual, 16 Oct 2019. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: exit load of 0.007% on day 1 falling to nil from day 7.
