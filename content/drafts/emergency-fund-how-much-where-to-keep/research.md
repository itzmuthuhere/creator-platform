# Research notes: Emergency fund, how much and where to keep it

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** Modern Approach For Emergency Fund Planning (Rahul Jain), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZNuuVJ37j2s. 11:06, ~3 years old, English auto-captions.
- **Second:** How much Emergency Fund do you need? Is 6x of your expenses Enough? (Zerodha Varsity), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vaxRsYGdIus. 6:15, ~1 year old, English.
- **Third:** Building an Emergency Fund in 2026 (bekifaayati), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GndemHAAECA. 13:00, ~9 months old, Hindi auto-captions.
- **Fourth:** Emergency Funds Explained (NDTV Profit, with Dilshad Billimoria), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kKg6MgYmiwQ. 11:26, ~2 months old, English auto-captions.

**Rahul Jain:** defines an emergency as serious, unexpected and urgent; a multiplier table (single earner: 6/12/18× monthly expenses for 0/1–2/3–4 dependents; dual earners: 3/6/9×); build it monthly, pausing SIPs if needed; property and equity funds don't count; keep it safe, withdrawable within 24 hours, roughly inflation-beating; split 50/50 between several FDs and several liquid funds; review every two years; refill after use.

**Varsity:** 6× is a starting point, not a rule; dependents change it; build it before or alongside investments; keep it in FD, savings or liquid funds, not equity; use a credit card for same-day needs while redemptions settle; refill it; don't spend it on lifestyle.

**bekifaayati:** don't multiply your full current spending; in a crisis you cut discretionary spending, so size the fund on what you'd still spend; peace of mind decides between 6 and 18 months.

**NDTV Profit:** include EMIs and insurance premiums; 12 months for single-earner households, 6 if the spouse works; build it gradually; sweep/flexi FDs and liquid funds; some liquid funds offer instant or ATM-style access; arbitrage funds have exit loads; a credit card can bridge until funds arrive; cashless insurance and claim timing in hospital emergencies.

Useful material across the four:
- Multipliers by earners and dependents (Jain, NDTV).
- Size on essentials, not total spending (bekifaayati).
- Include EMIs and premiums (NDTV).
- Where to park: savings, sweep FD, liquid funds; credit card as a bridge.
- Maintain: review, refill, don't raid.

## 6. Value the video doesn't give

- Essentials-based worked example (₹75,000 essentials vs ₹95,000 spending; 12 months = ₹9 lakh).
- Exact access mechanics: liquid-fund instant redemption per scheme per day at PAN level (non-demat only), SBI MOD sweep units and partial-break interest.
- Current returns measured from AMFI NAVs (6.5%) vs SBI FD 6.25% and savings 2.5%; ₹32,000/yr difference on ₹9 lakh.
- Tax parity between FD and liquid funds since April 2023; DICGC per-bank cap.
- Correction on "zero exit load".
