# Sources: Credit card balance transfer in India

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [Balance Transfer](https://www.sbicard.com/en/personal/benefits/lower-interest-option/balance-transfer.page), SBI Card. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 0% for 60 days; 1.7%/month (20.4% a year) for 180 days; NEFT within 3–4 working days.
- [Balance Transfer On EMI](https://www.sbicard.com/en/personal/benefits/lower-interest-option/balance-transfer-on-emi.page), SBI Card. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 3 months 10–17.5% a year, 6 months 12.5–20%; fee 0.75–1%; foreclosure 3%; "continue enjoying interest free period on purchases".
- [Balance Transfer terms and conditions](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/tnc/balance-transfer-tnc.pdf), SBI Card. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: ₹5,000 to 75% of available limit; finance charge up to 3.75%/month (45% a year) on unsecured cards; no interest-free period on purchases while a BT is outstanding; NEFT; cancellation only on the booking day.
- [Balance Transfer on EMI terms and conditions](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/tnc/balance-transfer-on-emi-tnc.pdf), SBI Card. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: rates tied to bureau score; reducing-balance method; ₹5,000 to 75% (its rate table is older, Dec 2024; the web page rates were used instead).
- [Most Important Terms & Conditions, Version 4.12](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/content/dam/hdfcbankpws/in/en/personal-banking/discover-products/cards/credit-cards/personal-mitc/mitc-in-english.pdf), HDFC Bank, Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: BT processing charge of 1% or ₹250, whichever is higher.

## Reputable secondary

- [Credit Card Charges And Fees](https://www.axis.bank.in/blogs/credit-card/credit-card-charges-and-fees), Axis Bank. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: typical balance transfer fee 1%–2%; card APR 24–49%.

## Source videos

- [Credit Card Balance Transfer | How To Transfer My Credit Card Balance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AYoQLuktxQM), BankBazaar (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the low rate is for a limited period; the transfer uses up the limit; standard rates on new purchases. Its "demand draft" step is outdated (SBI Card uses NEFT).
- [How Credit Card Balance Transfer Works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=86Dbx4KITjM), Money9 English (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: framing (pay the debt off faster; compare rate, tenure, fee).
- [Credit Card Balance Transfer In Reality](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7eJxomiP3v4), Card Buddhi (YouTube, Hindi). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the SBI Card booking flow for both types (pointer only).
