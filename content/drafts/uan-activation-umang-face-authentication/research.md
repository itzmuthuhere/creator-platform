## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** UMANG App से Face Scan करके UAN Activate कैसे करें? (ONLINE BY VIKRAM), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USsp_xXFLGo. ~2 months old; auto-translated. - **Third:** EPFO UAN Aadhaar face authentication update in UMANG (Gen Infopedia), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xgQm22GZ8as. ~5 months old, Tamil. Face authentication for already-activated UANs.
