# UAN activation in 2026: how to do it with UMANG face authentication

Your Universal Account Number (UAN) is the one number that ties together every PF account you've had. Until it's activated, you can't log in to EPFO's services: no passbook, no withdrawal, no KYC update, no transfer. UAN activation used to happen on EPFO's member website with an Aadhaar OTP. That route has now closed. Activation, and creating a new UAN, happens in the government's **UMANG** app, with a quick face scan matched against your Aadhaar.

This guide explains what changed, what you need, each step in UMANG, and what to do straight after activating.

## What changed

- **April 2025:** EPFO added face authentication services to UMANG, including creating and activating a UAN directly and activating an existing one, according to [KPMG's summary](https://www.in.kpmg.com/taxflashnews/KPMG-Flash-News-EPFO-issues-circular-for-UAN-Generation-via-UMANG-App.pdf) of the EPFO circulars.
- **1 August 2025:** new UANs could only be created this way. [Business Today reported](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/retirement-planning/story/epfo-aadhaar-face-authentication-mandatory-uan-generation-umang-app-488382-2025-08-07) that the employer-assisted route remained only for international workers and citizens of Nepal and Bhutan.
- **By September 2026:** activating an existing UAN on the member website had also been removed. [Karmactive reports](https://www.karmactive.com/epfo-uan-activation-umang-app-migration-aadhaar/) that UANs already activated aren't affected, and [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/uan-activation) now notes that the portal and Membership ID routes are discontinued.

The reason is uptake: Karmactive cites EPFO figures showing only about 35% of the 1.26 crore UANs allotted in 2024-25 were activated. The face scan pulls your details straight from Aadhaar, which also avoids the name and date-of-birth mismatches that used to block activation, as MadhanTalks' [Tamil video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hR3jSjA2pso) points out.

## Before you start

You need:

- **Your UAN** (from your payslip, appointment letter or HR). If you don't have one yet, see the section for new employees below.
- **Your Aadhaar number**, and **the mobile number linked to Aadhaar**, which must be working because the OTP goes there.
- **A smartphone** (the walkthroughs use Android) with the **UMANG** app and the **Aadhaar FaceRD** app installed from the Play Store. UMANG prompts you to install FaceRD if it's missing.
- Good light and a plain background for the face scan.

If your Aadhaar mobile number is out of date, update it first; the Aadhaar app allows this with face authentication.

## Activate your UAN in UMANG

ONLINE BY VIKRAM's [walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USsp_xXFLGo) shows these screens:

1. Open **UMANG**, register or log in with your mobile number.
2. Go to **All Services** and open **EPFO**.
3. Choose **UAN Activation** (it sits with the face authentication services).
4. Enter your **UAN**, your **Aadhaar number** and your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number**, tick the consent box and tap **Send OTP**.
5. Enter the OTP and submit.
6. Tap **Start scan**. The FaceRD app opens; allow camera access, look at the screen and blink when asked.
7. UMANG shows your name, photo, gender, date of birth and address from Aadhaar. Check them and submit.
8. You'll see a success message, and EPFO sends a **password by SMS** to your registered mobile.

## New employee without a UAN?

From August 2025, a new UAN is created by you, not your employer: in UMANG, choose the option for **UAN allotment and activation**, enter your Aadhaar and mobile number, and complete the same OTP and face scan. EPFO generates the UAN, activates it and sends it by SMS. Share it with your employer so your PF contributions go to it.

If you've worked before, use your existing UAN rather than creating a new one; one person should have one UAN for life.

## Right after activating

1. **Log in and change the password.** On EPFO's member website, sign in with your UAN and the SMS password, then set your own. Keep it in a [password manager](https://techpulzo.in/how-to-set-up-a-password-manager-in-15-minutes).
2. **Check your KYC.** Make sure your bank account, PAN and Aadhaar are linked and approved; claims fail without them.
3. **Add a nominee** (e-nomination) on the member website, so your family can claim without delay.
4. **Check your passbook** to confirm contributions are arriving.
5. **Merge old accounts.** If you see PF accounts from previous employers, transfer them to your current one.

Never share the SMS password or OTP with anyone who calls offering to "activate your PF" or "release your PF": EPFO doesn't ask for them by phone. Our guide to [how UPI fraud happens](https://techpulzo.in/how-upi-fraud-actually-happens-the-five-warning-signs) covers the same patterns.

## If it doesn't work

- **No OTP:** the mobile number you entered must be the one linked to Aadhaar, and it needs to receive SMS. A recent SIM replacement or eSIM switch can block SMS for 24 hours.
- **FaceRD app not found or face scan fails:** install or update Aadhaar FaceRD, try better lighting, remove glasses or a mask, and hold the phone at eye level.
- **"UAN already activated":** you can log in with your existing password, or use "Forgot password" on EPFO's website.
- **Details look wrong after the scan:** they come from Aadhaar. Correct Aadhaar first; PF details can then be aligned through your employer.
- **UMANG's EPFO services not loading:** they sometimes go down for maintenance; try again later.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I activate my UAN on the EPFO website?

Not any more. Activation through the member portal with an Aadhaar OTP has been discontinued; activation now happens in the UMANG app using Aadhaar face authentication. UANs already activated keep working, and you still log in on EPFO's website afterwards.

### Do I need my employer to activate my UAN?

No. You can activate an existing UAN yourself in UMANG. Since 1 August 2025, new UANs are also created by employees through UMANG's face authentication, except for international workers and Nepal and Bhutan citizens.

### What is the Aadhaar FaceRD app?

It's UIDAI's app that captures your live face and checks it against your Aadhaar record. UMANG uses it during UAN activation; you don't need to open it separately.

### What if my Aadhaar mobile number has changed?

Update the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar first, because the OTP goes to that number. Then activate your UAN in UMANG.

### What should I do after activating my UAN?

Log in to EPFO's member website with the SMS password and change it, complete your bank, PAN and Aadhaar KYC, add a nominee, check your passbook, and transfer any older PF accounts to your current UAN.

## The short version

Install UMANG and Aadhaar FaceRD, keep your Aadhaar-linked phone handy, and use the UAN Activation option with a face scan. Then change the password and finish your KYC so the account is ready when you need it.
