# Sources: Repo rate change and your home loan EMI

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [External Benchmark based Lending Rate](https://sbi.bank.in/web/interest-rates/external-benchmark-based-lending-rate), State Bank of India, last updated 12 Dec 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: EBLR 7.90% + CRP + BSP and RLLR 7.50% + CRP from 15 Dec 2025.
- [FAQs: Circular dated August 18, 2023 on Reset of Floating Interest Rate on EMI based Personal Loans](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/Upload/English/FAQs/PDFs/FAQRFIR10012025.pdf), RBI, updated 1 Oct 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: options at reset (higher EMI / longer tenure / mix; fixed rate where offered, inserted Oct 2025; prepay); communication duties; quarterly statements; applies to existing borrowers and to internal-benchmark loans.

## Reputable secondary

- [RBI makes External Benchmark Based Interest Rate mandatory from October 1, 2019](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2019/09/07/rbi-makes-external-benchmark-based-interest-rate-mandatory-for-certain-categories-of-loans-from-october-1-2019/), SCC Online, 7 Sep 2019. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the mandate for new floating retail loans from 1 Oct 2019; choice of benchmark; credit risk premium changes only on a substantial change in credit assessment.
- [Home Loans from October 1, 2019, to be linked to External Benchmark](https://personalfinanceplan.in/retail-home-loans-external-benchmark/), Personal Finance Plan. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: reset at least once in 3 months; operating-cost spread changes once in 3 years.
- [Fixed rate flip on interest rate reset now optional](https://vinodkothari.com/2025/10/rbi-eases-interest-rate-norms-for-lenders/), Vinod Kothari Consultants, Oct 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: banks may reduce non-credit spread components before 3 years; fixed-rate switch optional; effective 1 Oct 2025.
- [RBI Policy October 2026: Repo Rate, Inflation & EMI Outlook](https://www.gopocket.in/news/rbi-policy-october-2026-repo-rate-inflation-emi-outlook), GoPocket, 29 Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: repo at 5.25%, held a fourth time in Aug 2026; inflation rising (the article states this generally).

- [RBI Repo Rate Cut 2025 Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rZBlRQn0Y64), Yuvarani (YouTube, Tamil). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the 2025 cut sequence (6.5% → 5.25%) and the bank-to-borrower framing.
