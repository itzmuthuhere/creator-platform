# eSIM activation in India: how to switch on Jio, Airtel or Vi

An eSIM is a SIM built into your phone, so there's no plastic card to insert or lose. All three private operators let you convert your existing number to an eSIM without visiting a store: Jio through the MyJio app, Airtel and Vi by SMS. The process takes a couple of hours and ends with a QR code you scan. There's one catch to plan for: after activation, SMS on your number, including bank and UPI OTPs, is blocked for 24 hours.

This guide covers eSIM activation in India for each operator, what to check first, and what changes once your number lives on an eSIM.

## Before you start

- **Your phone must support eSIM.** On iPhones, [Apple says](https://support.apple.com/en-in/118669) that means the iPhone XS, XS Max, XR or later. Many recent Android phones support it too; check your phone's settings for an option to add an eSIM.
- **Find your EID.** This is the 32-digit identifier of the phone's eSIM chip. You'll find it in Settings under About phone (Android) or General > About (iPhone). Vi asks for it in the activation SMS, and Jio asks for it in the app.
- **Use Wi-Fi.** You'll need an internet connection to download the eSIM profile, and your mobile data will drop when the old SIM stops working.
- **Check your email.** The QR code goes to the email address registered with your operator, so make sure it's current.
- **Pick a quiet day.** Because of the 24-hour SMS bar, don't switch just before you need an OTP.

## Jio: through the MyJio app

Jio's [official walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mK83YjNXocI) sets out the steps:

1. Open **MyJio**, logged in with the number you want to convert, and search for **eSIM**. Choose **Switch to eSIM**.
2. Enter your email address and verify it with the OTP sent there. The QR code will come to this address.
3. Keep your Jio SIM in the same phone for **SIM authentication**, then enter the **EID** of the phone that will hold the eSIM (this phone or a new one).
4. Enter the OTP sent to your Jio number to submit the request.
5. Wait about **two hours**. Your current SIM keeps working during this cooling period.
6. Answer the **confirmation call** (IVR) on your Jio number and follow the prompt. If you miss it, Jio says to send **RECALL** to 199 for a callback.
7. Install the eSIM on Wi-Fi: on iPhones and Samsung phones you can tap the profile notification; otherwise scan the QR code from your email using the phone's "add eSIM" option.

When the eSIM activates, your physical SIM shows no network. Jio can also issue a **new** connection or port your number from another operator directly as an eSIM, with Aadhaar-based self-KYC in the app, as its [second video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8hzs4iNOe0A) shows.

## Airtel: by SMS to 121

According to [Airtel's guide](https://www.airtel.in/blog/postpaid/how-to-activate-esim-convert-airtel-sim-to-esim/):

1. From your Airtel number, send an SMS to **121**: `eSIM <your registered email address>` (the word eSIM, a space, then the email).
2. Reply **1** to confirm when asked.
3. You'll get a call asking for your **consent**; give it.
4. You'll receive the **QR code by email**. Scan it in your phone's settings to add the eSIM.
5. Activation takes **around two hours**. Your physical SIM stays active until then.

If the QR code doesn't arrive, Airtel says postpaid users can send **NOSIM** to 121.

## Vi: by SMS to 199

[Vi's eSIM page](https://www.myvi.in/esim-activation) asks for more detail in the first message:

1. From your Vi number, send an SMS to **199**: `eSIM <32-digit EID> <1 for iPhone or 2 for Android> <email>`
2. Within **15 minutes**, reply **ESIMY** to confirm.
3. Answer the verification call and press **1**.
4. You'll get the QR code from esim@vodafoneidea.com. Scan it to install the eSIM.

Vi says eSIM isn't available in some circles: Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Odisha, Assam and the North East.

## The 24-hour SMS and OTP bar

Both Jio and Vi state that, under government rules, **SMS on your number is blocked for 24 hours after eSIM activation**. That includes OTPs for banking, UPI, email logins and deliveries. Calls and data work as usual.

So:

- Don't switch the day before a payment, a flight check-in or anything else that needs an OTP.
- If you use your phone number for two-step login on important accounts, make sure you have a backup method (an authenticator app or backup codes) before you start.
- Be wary of anyone who contacts you during this window claiming to help with "eSIM activation" and asking for an OTP. The restriction exists because fraudsters use SIM replacements to take over people's numbers. Our guide to [how UPI fraud happens](https://techpulzo.in/how-upi-fraud-actually-happens-the-five-warning-signs) covers the warning signs.

## eSIM: what you gain and what changes

**Gains**

- Nothing to lose or damage, and a thief can't simply pull out your SIM and use it in another phone.
- Many phones support an eSIM alongside a physical SIM, which makes it easy to keep a second number or add a travel plan.

**Things to know**

- **Never delete the eSIM profile** from settings unless you mean to. All three operators warn that this removes your service, and you'd need a new eSIM.
- **Changing phones takes a step.** You can't just move a card. You request the eSIM again with the new phone's EID (Jio lets you do this from the app), or use your phone's transfer feature if your operator supports it.
- **If your phone breaks,** you'll need your operator to issue a new eSIM or a physical SIM, so keep your registered email up to date.

Your plan and number stay the same, so there's no need to change your recharge; if you're reviewing it anyway, our comparison of [prepaid and postpaid costs](https://techpulzo.in/prepaid-vs-postpaid-mobile-plans-which-actually-costs-less-over-a-year) may help.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I activate an eSIM on Airtel?

Send an SMS to 121 from your Airtel number with the word eSIM, a space and your registered email address. Reply 1 to confirm, give consent on the call you receive, then scan the QR code sent to your email. Activation takes about two hours.

### What is the EID and where do I find it?

The EID is the 32-digit identifier of your phone's built-in eSIM chip. You'll find it in Settings under About phone on Android, or General > About on an iPhone. Vi needs it in the activation SMS, and Jio asks for it in the MyJio app.

### Why am I not getting OTPs after switching to eSIM?

SMS is blocked for 24 hours after eSIM activation under government rules, as Jio and Vi both state. Calls and data work normally. OTPs will arrive again once the 24 hours have passed.

### Will my number or plan change when I switch to eSIM?

No. Your number, plan and balance stay the same. Only the SIM changes from a physical card to a profile stored in your phone.

### Can I move my eSIM to a new phone?

Not by moving a card. Request a new eSIM for the new phone (Jio lets you enter the new phone's EID in the app), or use your phone's transfer feature where your operator supports it. Don't delete the old profile until the new one works.

## Plan the timing

Check compatibility, note your EID, connect to Wi-Fi, and pick a day when you won't need OTPs for 24 hours. The rest is a few minutes of SMS or app steps and a two-hour wait.
