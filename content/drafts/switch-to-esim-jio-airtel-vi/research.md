# Research notes: How to switch to an eSIM on Jio, Airtel or Vi

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** Switch to eSIM via MyJio App in 3 Steps (Jio, official), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mK83YjNXocI. ~11 months old. MyJio → search eSIM → verify email by OTP (QR comes there) → SIM authentication with Jio SIM in the device → enter EID (same or new device) → OTP → request submitted; ~2-hour cooling period with old SIM working; IVR call from 022-35072222 to confirm (SMS RECALL to 199 for callback; press 1 to cancel); install via profile/QR on Wi-Fi; old SIM loses network; SMS unavailable 24 h after activation "as per government guidelines"; don't delete the eSIM profile.
- **Second:** Get Jio eSIM online with self-KYC or port your number (Jio, official), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8hzs4iNOe0A. ~2 months old, Hindi. New connection/port as eSIM through MyJio with Aadhaar-based self-KYC, live photo + 3-sec video; eSIM-compatible device (e.g. iOS 26+ mentioned for Apple).
- **Third:** eSIM மாறுவது லாபமா? நஷ்டமா? (Sathiyam News), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1M898JAdPUE. ~1 month old, Tamil; captions unusable (auto-translation garbled). Pros/cons framing only.
