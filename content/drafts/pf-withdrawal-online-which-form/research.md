# Research notes: How to withdraw PF online, and which form to use

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** EPFO 3.0 Portal: PF and EPS Withdrawal 2026 New rules explained (eLearningGang), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oC-Qoef04R4. ~2 months old, Hindi. Portal changes: Form 10D error when the EPS exit reason is missing; Form 10C blocked if an old member ID is "due for transfer"; 36-month wait for 10C; Form 31 minimum payout ₹1,000; reasons cut to five with the eligible amount shown (about 75% of employee + employer share); Form 19 pays only the primary member ID's balance; Form 121 entered online for TDS.
- **Second:** PF Withdrawal Process Online | Form 31 | New Process 2026 (MySimpleGuide), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hh0j1qr6jbQ. 4 days old, Hindi. Steps: member portal login with OTP, check KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, bank), File claim, verify bank account, choose Form 31, purpose, amount within the eligible limit, address, Aadhaar OTP, submit.
- **Third:** EPFO 3.0 Full PF Withdrawal New Process (Som Techinfo), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XPOEUKJD6Qs. 1 day old, Hindi. Full settlement: Form 19 then Form 10C separately; transfer old member IDs first or 10C is rejected; status tracking.
