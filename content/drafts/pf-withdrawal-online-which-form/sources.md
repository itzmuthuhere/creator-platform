- [Form 15G / 15H for PF Withdrawal Replaced by Form 121](https://kustodian.life/provident-fund/epfo-latest-news/form-15g-15h-for-pf-withdrawal-replaced-by-form-121-complete-2026-epf-tds-guide) — Kustodian, Jun 2026. - [No TDS for PF withdrawals of up to Rs 50,000 from June 1](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/pti-stories/no-tds-for-pf-withdrawals-of-up-to-rs-50k-from-june-1-116053001393_1.html) — Business Standard (PTI), 30 May 2016. - [Tax on EPF withdrawals: Know what rules say](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/tax-on-epf-withdrawals-know-what-rules-say-about-deduction-exemption-124091100458_1.html) — Business Standard, 11 Sep 2024. - [Government Ratifies 8.25% EPF Interest Rate for FY 2025-26](https://www.psuconnect.in/news/government-ratifies-8-25-epf-interest-rate-for-fy-2025-26-interest-likely-to-be-credited-to-over-7-crore-members-this-month) — PSU Connect, 19 Jun 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: 8.25% for 2025-26.
