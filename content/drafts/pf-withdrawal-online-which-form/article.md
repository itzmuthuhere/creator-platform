# How to withdraw PF online in 2026, and which form to use

Much of what you'll read about PF withdrawal online is now out of date. The Employees' Provident Fund Scheme, 2026 replaced the old 1952 scheme, and its withdrawal rules took effect on 29 June 2026, as [DD News reported](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/govt-notifies-new-epf-scheme-with-revised-partial-withdrawal-norms/). Some limits got more generous: you can now take out part of your employer's share too. Others got stricter: you must leave 25% in the account, and you wait much longer after leaving a job before you can close it.

This guide explains which form fits your situation, how much you can withdraw, when tax is deducted, and the online steps, under the rules as of October 2026.

## What changed in 2026

- **A 25% floor.** You must always keep 25% of your eligible EPF balance in the account. DD News gives the example plainly: on ₹1 lakh, ₹25,000 stays and up to ₹75,000 can be withdrawn. This covers both your share and your employer's.
- **Fewer, broader reasons.** Partial withdrawals are now grouped under a short list of purposes, and you need 12 months of membership for any of them.
- **Longer waits after leaving a job.** EPFO's board approved extending the wait for a full PF settlement after leaving employment from 2 months to **12 months**, and for withdrawing the pension (EPS) amount from 2 months to **36 months**, as [Upstox reported](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/latest-updates/8-major-changes-in-epf-partial-withdrawal-rules-and-premature-final-settlement-approved-by-epfo/article-182969/) after the October 2025 meeting.
- **A ₹1,000 minimum.** You can't withdraw less than ₹1,000 to your bank account.
- **New tax form.** Form 121 replaced Forms 15G and 15H for TDS declarations from April 2026, under the new Income-tax Act.

## Which form for which situation

| Your situation | Form on the portal | What it pays |
|---|---|---|
| Still employed, need money for illness, education, marriage, housing or another need | **Form 31** (partial withdrawal, "advance") | Up to the eligible amount for that purpose, after keeping 25% aside |
| Left your job and want to close the PF account | **Form 19** (final settlement) | The PF balance of your primary member ID, once the waiting period is over |
| Left your job with under 10 years of pension service | **Form 10C** (pension withdrawal) | Your EPS contributions as a lump sum, after the 36-month wait |
| 10 or more years of pension service, at pension age | **Form 10D** (monthly pension) | A monthly pension instead of a lump sum |

*Forms 10C and 10D apply to the Employees' Pension Scheme. SBI Life's [Form 10C guide](https://www.sbilife.co.in/blogs/finance/epf-form-10c) explains the 10-year line.*

## How much you can take out

Under the 2026 scheme, the reasons for a partial withdrawal, as listed by [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/latest-updates/epf-scheme-2026-6-reasons-for-partial-withdrawals-how-much-you-can-get/article-196231/), are:

| Purpose | How often |
|---|---|
| Illness (you or family) | No limit stated |
| Education (you or family) | Up to 10 times during membership |
| Marriage (you or family) | Up to 5 times |
| Housing: buying, building, a plot, repaying a home loan, repairs | Up to 5 times |
| Special circumstances, with no explanation needed | Twice a financial year |
| Leaving employment before 12 months | Once |

For each, the limit is up to 100% of your **eligible balance**, which the scheme defines as what's left after setting aside the compulsory 25%.

A worked example: your passbook shows ₹4,00,000 in EPF (employee plus employer share). ₹1,00,000 must stay. For a medical emergency you could withdraw up to ₹3,00,000. In practice, the portal shows the eligible amount for the purpose you select, and you can claim anything up to it. A tutorial by eLearningGang shows the form now displaying this figure automatically, about 75% of the combined balance.

If you're taking money out for a house, our guide to [how much home loan you can afford](https://techpulzo.in/how-much-home-loan-you-can-actually-afford) is worth reading first; PF used for a down payment is retirement money you won't get back.

## After leaving a job: Form 19 and Form 10C

When you stop working, two separate claims close your account: **Form 19** for the PF money and **Form 10C** for the pension contributions, if you have under 10 years of pension service. Som Techinfo's walkthrough files them one after the other.

Three things trip people up:

1. **The waiting periods.** Under the board's decision, now in force according to [Bajaj Finserv's August 2026 guide](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/investments/epf-or-pf-withdrawal-rules), a full PF settlement needs 12 months without a job, and the pension amount 36 months. Some guides also describe an earlier, partial release (75% after a month without a job); sources disagree on whether that still applies, so go by the eligibility the portal shows you.
2. **Old employers' accounts.** Form 19 pays the balance of your *primary* member ID, the latest one. eLearningGang shows Form 10C refusing to proceed while an older ID is "due for transfer". Transfer old accounts into the current one first.
3. **Exit details.** If your last employer hasn't recorded your date of leaving and reason, claims fail. You can mark your own exit on the member portal if the employer doesn't, but check both the EPF and EPS exit reasons appear in your service history.

If you're just changing jobs, don't withdraw at all: transfer the account. Transfers are tax-free and keep your service history, which matters for both the 5-year tax rule and the 10-year pension rule.

## Tax: when TDS is deducted

TDS is deducted on a PF withdrawal only if **both** of these are true:

- your continuous service is **under 5 years**, and
- the withdrawal is **₹50,000 or more**.

That threshold has been ₹50,000 since 1 June 2016, as [Business Standard reported](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/pti-stories/no-tds-for-pf-withdrawals-of-up-to-rs-50k-from-june-1-116053001393_1.html) at the time. Some guides, including a 2024 Business Standard explainer and Bajaj Finserv's current page, still say ₹30,000.

When TDS does apply, a [2024 Business Standard explainer](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/tax-on-epf-withdrawals-know-what-rules-say-about-deduction-exemption-124091100458_1.html) puts it at 10% if you've given your PAN, and 34.608% if you haven't. If your total income for the year is below the taxable limit, you can avoid TDS by submitting **Form 121**, which [replaced Forms 15G and 15H](https://kustodian.life/provident-fund/epfo-latest-news/form-15g-15h-for-pf-withdrawal-replaced-by-form-121-complete-2026-epf-tds-guide) from April 2026. eLearningGang shows the claim form now lets you enter Form 121 details directly. Form 121 stops the deduction; it doesn't make the withdrawal tax-free if your income is taxable.

Example: you leave after three years and settle ₹2.4 lakh. With your PAN on record and no Form 121, about ₹24,000 is deducted. If your income for the year is low, you can claim it back when you file your return.

## Step by step: filing a claim online

The sequence below is how MySimpleGuide's recent tutorial and the other walkthroughs show it on EPFO's unified member portal; button names vary slightly between the portal's layouts.

1. **Log in** to the member portal with your UAN and password, then the OTP sent to your registered mobile.
2. **Check KYC.** Under your profile, confirm Aadhaar, PAN and bank account show as verified. A missing PAN means higher TDS; a missing bank account means the claim can't be paid.
3. Open the claim option: **File Claim** on the newer dashboard, or **Online Services → Claim (Form-31, 19, 10C & 10D)** on the older menu.
4. **Verify your bank account** by entering its number when asked, and confirm.
5. **Choose the form** and the member ID.
6. **For Form 31:** pick the purpose. The eligible amount appears; enter the amount you want, at least ₹1,000.
7. **Enter your address**, accept the declaration, and **request an Aadhaar OTP**.
8. **Submit** with the OTP. Note the claim number, and track it under the claim status option.

Bajaj Finserv reports that eligible online claims are now targeted for settlement within three working days. DD News reports that EPFO has tested withdrawals through UPI and plans WhatsApp services, but neither was described as live.

## Common errors and fixes

| Message or problem | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Amount box won't accept your figure | It exceeds the eligible amount (after the 25% floor) | Enter the amount shown or less |
| Form 10C shows an error | An old member ID is "due for transfer", or the 36-month wait hasn't passed | Transfer old IDs first; check the date shown |
| Form 10D: reason for leaving not marked | EPS exit reason missing in service history | Ask the employer to update it, or mark exit on the portal |
| Claim rejected for bank details | Bank account not verified in KYC | Update and get KYC approved, then refile |
| No Aadhaar OTP | Aadhaar mobile differs from EPFO mobile | Use the number linked to Aadhaar; update if needed |

Finally, EPFO never asks for fees or OTPs over the phone to "release" PF. Anyone who does is attempting fraud; our guide to [how UPI frauds work](https://techpulzo.in/how-upi-fraud-actually-happens-the-five-warning-signs) covers the warning signs.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I withdraw 100% of my PF while still working?

No. Under the 2026 scheme you must keep 25% of your eligible balance in the account, so a partial withdrawal can take at most what's left after that. The "100%" in news reports refers to 100% of the eligible balance, which already excludes the 25%. The full balance is paid only at final settlement, for example on retirement.

### How soon after leaving a job can I withdraw my full PF?

Under the board decision now in force, a full settlement of the PF balance is allowed after 12 months without a job, and the pension (EPS) amount after 36 months. Before that, consider transferring the account to a new employer instead. The member portal shows what you're currently eligible to claim.

### Do I need to give a reason for a PF advance?

You choose a purpose on Form 31: illness, education, marriage, housing or special circumstances. Under special circumstances, DD News reports that no further explanation is required, though that category is limited to twice a financial year. You need at least 12 months of membership for any partial withdrawal.

### Is PF withdrawal taxable?

Not if you have five or more years of continuous service; earlier withdrawals of ₹50,000 or more face TDS. Even with TDS, the money is only taxed if your total income is taxable; you can file a return to claim a refund. Transfers between employers aren't taxable.

### Which is better after leaving a job: withdraw or transfer?

Transfer, unless you need the money. Transferring keeps your service continuous, which protects you from TDS at the five-year mark and counts towards the ten years needed for an EPS pension. It also keeps earning interest: [8.25% for 2025–26](https://www.psuconnect.in/news/government-ratifies-8-25-epf-interest-rate-for-fy-2025-26-interest-likely-to-be-credited-to-over-7-crore-members-this-month).

## Withdraw for needs, transfer for job changes

The 2026 rules make PF easier to use in a genuine emergency and harder to drain completely. Use Form 31 when you need money while working, keep old accounts transferred so the forms work, and treat a full withdrawal as a decision about your retirement, not just your next month. Because these rules changed recently, check the eligible amount the portal shows before you plan around any figure in this article.
