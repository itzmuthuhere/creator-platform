# Research notes: How to file ITR-1 online (salaried)

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** ITR Filing 2026-27 for Salary Person | Step by Step ITR-1 (FinTaxPro), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OC4iEVs0jLk. ~2 months old. Filing is mandatory above the basic exemption limit (₹4 lakh, new regime); walkthrough from Form 16 Part B: start new filing, ITR-1, personal info (secondary address quirk), employer category, filing section 139(1), regime, all bank accounts, salary vs Form 16, the removed "any other" exemption, other sources and exempt income (PF, Sukanya), LTCG 112A up to ₹1.25 lakh in ITR-1, deductions (employer NPS only in new regime), tax paid matched to 26AS and AIS, validation, Aadhaar OTP e-verification; warns against fake allowances.
- **Second:** How to File ITR-1 Online (AY 2026-27) (CA Shweta Verma), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1wAyr9zfd8o. ~4 months old. Download 26AS and AIS first; AIS is updated by about 15 June; report everything AIS shows; all bank accounts and one for refund; up to two house properties in ITR-1; self-occupied loan interest only in old regime.
- **Third:** File ITR after Due Date | Belated ITR Filing | AY 2026-27 (ClearTax), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lFZbbgHQPTI. ~2 months old. Late fee ₹1,000 (income up to ₹5 lakh) / up to ₹5,000; product walkthrough.
- **Fourth:** ITR 1 & ITR 2 Filing after Due Date Without Late Fees (Tax Filing School), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kmnr_G5pkjs. ~2 months old, Hindi. After the due date the portal selects 139(4) and only the new regime; no late fee if income is below the exemption limit; suggests ITR-3/4 to avoid the fee (not used: questionable).
