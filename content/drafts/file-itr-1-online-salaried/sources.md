# Sources: How to file ITR-1 online (salaried)

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.
Format: `- [Page title](url) — Publisher. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD. Used for: what it supported.`

## Primary / official

- [File ITR-1 (Sahaj) Online FAQs](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/ITR1-FAQ) — Income Tax Department. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: ITR-1 eligibility for AY 2026-27 (₹50 lakh, up to two house properties, LTCG under 112A up to ₹1.25 lakh, agricultural income up to ₹5,000) and exclusions; e-verification within 30 days; belated filing allowed with a fee.
- [FAQs on New Tax vs Old Tax Regime](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/new-tax-vs-old-tax-regime-faqs) — Income Tax Department. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: individuals without business income can switch every year, with the choice made by the due date under section 139(1).

## Reputable secondary

- [ITR Filing Last Date FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)](https://cleartax.in/s/due-date-tax-filing) — ClearTax. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: 31 July 2026 due date (not extended); belated returns until 31 December 2026; 234F fee (₹1,000 / ₹5,000); 234A interest; losses not carried forward; ITR-U window.
- [Why You May Be Forced into New Tax Regime if you missed ITR Deadline](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/forced-new-tax-regime-missed-itr-deadline.html) — TaxGuru (Dr Suresh Surana), 2 Oct 2025. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: belated returns under 139(4) can't opt for the old regime.

## Source videos

- [ITR Filing 2026-27 for Salary Person | Step by Step ITR-1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OC4iEVs0jLk) — FinTaxPro (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: step sequence; matching with Form 16, 26AS and AIS; caution on fake allowances.
- [How to File ITR-1 Online (AY 2026-27)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1wAyr9zfd8o) — CA Shweta Verma (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: download 26AS and AIS first; AIS update timing (attributed); bank account and house property details.
- [File ITR after Due Date | Belated ITR Filing | AY 2026-27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lFZbbgHQPTI) — ClearTax (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: belated-return framing and late-fee amounts (verified).
- [ITR 1 & ITR 2 Filing after Due Date](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kmnr_G5pkjs) — Tax Filing School (YouTube, Hindi). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: the portal locking belated returns to section 139(4) and the new regime (verified). Its suggestion to use ITR-3/4 to avoid the fee is not used.
