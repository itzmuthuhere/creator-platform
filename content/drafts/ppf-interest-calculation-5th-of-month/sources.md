# Sources: How PPF interest is calculated

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.
Format: `- [Page title](url) — Publisher. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD. Used for: what it supported.`

## Primary / official

- [FAQ Public Provident Fund](https://sbi.bank.in/web/faq-s/faq-public-provident-fund) — State Bank of India. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: no restriction on number of instalments; ₹500–₹1.5 lakh in multiples of ₹50; interest on the lower balance from the 5th to month end; credited on 31 March; withdrawal, loan and extension rules.
- [Public Provident Fund Scheme, 2019 – Detailed Analysis](https://taxguru.in/finance/public-provident-fund-scheme-2019-detailed-analysis.html) — TaxGuru, reproducing the scheme notified as G.S.R. 915(E), 12 Dec 2019. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: scheme wording on deposits, the "lowest balance between the close of the fifth day and the end of the month" rule, yearly crediting, 1% lower interest on premature closure.
