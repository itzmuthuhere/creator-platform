# Sources: How much term insurance cover you need

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.
Format: `- [Page title](url) — Publisher. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD. Used for: what it supported.`

## Primary / official

- [Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' interests 2024 (document page)](https://irdai.gov.in/document-detail?documentId=5625747) — IRDAI. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: confirming the circular is IRDAI's current issued document (English PDF is 87 MB; text read from the full copy below).
- [Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2024](https://www.caalley.com/irdai_mc/MC_Protection_of_Policyholders_interests_2024.pdf) — IRDAI, 5 Sep 2024 (copy hosted by CAalley). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: death claims settled within 15 days of intimation, 45 days if investigation is warranted; interest at bank rate + 2% for delays; no rejection for delayed intimation or want of documents; no policy contestable after three years.

## Reputable secondary

- [How to Choose the Right Sum Assured Under a Term Plan](https://www.axismaxlife.com/blog/term-insurance/how-to-choose-the-right-sum-assured-under-a-term-plan) — Axis Max Life Insurance, modified 11 Jul 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: insurer guidance of 10–20× income and the four calculation methods (HLV, expense replacement, income replacement, thumb rule).
- [Maximum Sum Assured in Term Insurance Explained](https://joinditto.in/articles/life-insurance/maximum-sum-assured-in-term-insurance/) — Ditto Insurance (broker), updated 24 Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: reference table of maximum cover multiples by age; limits set by each insurer's board-approved underwriting policy.

## Source videos

- [Don't Buy Term Life Insurance Before Watching This](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zYu-fO9wwm0) — Ditto Insurance (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: 20–30× rule (attributed); premium indication; group cover lapsing; tenure to 60–70; disclosure. Its claim timelines are corrected.
- [Term Insurance: How Much Coverage Do You Need at Every Age? ft. Manish Alagh](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=euQ-PRupOyY) — Smart Up (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: 10× at 30 / 5× near retirement (attributed); adding cover as liabilities grow.
- [Term Insurance | Term Life Insurance | Life Insurance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x2O_cXMoAEE) — XY Axis Education (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: cover only while dependents rely on you; premium difference for cover to 99 (attributed illustration).
