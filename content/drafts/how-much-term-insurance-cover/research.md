# Research notes: How much term insurance cover you need

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** Don't Buy Term Life Insurance Before Watching This (Ditto Insurance), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zYu-fO9wwm0. 15:20, ~1 year old, English auto-captions. Ditto is an insurance broker.
- **Second:** Term Insurance: How Much Coverage Do You Need at Every Age? ft. Manish Alagh (Smart Up), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=euQ-PRupOyY. 4:23, ~1 year old.
- **Third:** Term Insurance | Term Life Insurance (XY Axis Education), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x2O_cXMoAEE. 26:17, ~3 months old.
- Dropped: CA Rahul Malodia (b0OaRLLC9Ug); captions cover only the intro.

**Ditto:** what term insurance is, exclusions, variants (level, return of premium, increasing, decreasing, homemaker, employer group cover), cover rule of 20–30× income with a 6% payout illustration, adjust for loans, education, inflation; tenure to 60–70; built-in features and riders; application and medicals; claims process with "30 days, 120 with investigation"; mistakes (under-insuring, short tenure, non-disclosure).

**Smart Up (Manish Alagh):** about 10× income around age 30, at least 5× near retirement; step-up or new policies as income and liabilities grow; no need once retirement corpus is enough.

**XY Axis:** cover only while dependents rely on you (65 rather than 99/100); return-of-premium plans cost much more for the same cover; payout options (lump sum, monthly, mix); regular vs limited pay.

Useful material:
- Rules of thumb (10× vs 20–30×) that disagree.
- Factors: loans, education, inflation, dependents' timeline.
- Tenure until dependents are independent.
- Employer group cover lapses with the job.
- Claims and non-disclosure.

**Verdict:** PROCEED.

**Chosen angle:** The popular multiples (10×, 20–30×) disagree by a factor of three for the same person. A short needs-based calculation gives a defensible number, shows which inputs move it, and explains why a level cover bought young still roughly fits later. Then the practical limits: what insurers will sell relative to income and age, what employer cover is worth, and the claim rules that make honest disclosure matter.
