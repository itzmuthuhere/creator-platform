# Sources: How to read a mutual fund factsheet

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.
Format: `- [Page title](url) — Publisher. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD. Used for: what it supported.`

## Primary / official

- [PPFAS Mutual Fund Factsheet, August 2026](https://amc.ppfas.com/downloads/factsheet/2026/ppfas-mf-factsheet-for-August-2026.pdf) — PPFAS Mutual Fund. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the worked example (Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund: benchmark, base expense ratio, returns, rolling returns, risk ratios, turnover, exit load, risk-free rate).
- [Disclosure of Risk adjusted Return – Information Ratio (IR) for Mutual Fund Schemes](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jan-2025/disclosure-of-risk-adjusted-return-information-ratio-ir-for-mutual-fund-schemes-_90928.html) — SEBI circular SEBI/HO/IMD/IMD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2025/6, 17 Jan 2025 (PDF read). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: IR definition, formula, Tier 1 benchmark, daily disclosure, equity-oriented schemes only.
- [Understanding the Riskometer](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/riskometer.html) — SEBI investor website. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: six risk levels; riskometer shown in factsheets.
- [TER of MF Schemes](https://www.amfiindia.com/ter-of-mf-schemes) — AMFI, September 2026 data via the page's API. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: Parag Parikh Flexi Cap total TER (1.30% regular, 0.69% direct) vs base expense ratio.

## Reputable secondary

- [Product Labeling in Mutual Fund schemes – Risk-o-meter](https://taxguru.in/sebi/product-labeling-mutual-fund-schemes-risk-o-meter.html) — TaxGuru (text of SEBI circular SEBI/HO/IMD/DF3/CIR/P/2020/197, 5 Oct 2020). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: monthly evaluation; disclosure within 10 days of month end; unitholder notification; effective 1 Jan 2021.
