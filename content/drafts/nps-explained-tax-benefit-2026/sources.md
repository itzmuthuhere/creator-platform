## Primary / official

- [PFRDA (Exits and Withdrawals under the NPS) Regulations, 2015 (last amended 16 December 2025)](https://www.pfrda.org.in/documents/33652/184762/PFRDA+(Exits+and+Withdrawals+under+the+NPS)+Regulations,+2015+%5BLast+amended+on+16+December+2025%5D.pdf) — PFRDA. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: exit after 15 years (not for employment-linked schemes); ₹8 lakh and ₹8–12 lakh bands; deferment to 85; partial withdrawals (25% of own contributions, four times before 60, four-year gap).

## Reputable secondary

- [NPS Withdrawal Rules 2025: Major Changes](https://www.proteantech.in/articles/nps-withdrawal-rules-2025-changes-december/) — Protean (an NPS central recordkeeping agency), Dec 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 80% lump sum / 20% annuity above ₹12 lakh; loan against NPS up to 25% of own contributions.
- [NPS Withdrawal Rules 2026: Bigger Lump Sum, Better Retirement](https://www.proteantech.in/articles/lump-sum-revolution-with-nps/) — Protean. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: only 60% exempt under section 10(12A); extra 20% may be taxed.
- [New NPS withdrawal rules: 80% lumpsum allowed but only 60% tax-free](https://1finance.co.in/blog/new-nps-withdrawal-rules-taxation/) — 1 Finance, 25 May 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: post-Budget confirmation that the extra 20% is taxable at slab rates; annuity taxable when received.
- [National Pension Scheme (NPS): Tax Benefits, Eligibility, Withdrawal](https://cleartax.in/s/nps-national-pension-scheme) — ClearTax. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: ₹50,000 extra deduction only in the old regime; employer contribution 14% (new) / 10% (old); government subscribers 60/40; Tier II tax-saver deduction with 3-year lock-in.
