# How to check your EPF balance and download the passbook

The quickest way to check your EPF balance is a missed call to 9966044425 from the mobile number registered with EPFO; a text message with your balance arrives a few seconds later. For the full passbook, showing every monthly contribution, the interest credited and any withdrawals, you need EPFO's passbook site or the UMANG app.

This guide covers all four methods, what each one needs, how to download the passbook as a PDF, and what to do when a method doesn't work. It reflects EPFO's systems as of October 2026, after the "EPFO 3.0" migration in mid-2026 and the move of EPFO's main website to epfo.gov.in.

## Before you start

Three things decide which methods will work for you:

- **An activated UAN.** Your Universal Account Number (UAN) is on your salary slip or available from HR. It has to be activated once before you can log in anywhere. You can do this on EPFO's member portal, linked from [epfo.gov.in](https://www.epfo.gov.in), or in the UMANG app, which now [activates UANs using face authentication](https://web.umang.gov.in/landing/department/epfo.html).
- **A registered mobile number.** The missed-call and SMS services only respond to the number linked to your UAN.
- **KYC linked to your UAN.** Aadhaar, PAN and bank details need to be seeded (linked) for the SMS and missed-call services, as [ClearTax's guide](https://cleartax.in/s/pf-balance-check) explains.

One exception: if your employer runs its own PF trust (an "exempted establishment", common at some large companies), your balance isn't held by EPFO and won't show up on EPFO's portal. Your employer's trust or HR provides the statement instead.

## The four ways, compared

| Method | What you need | What you get | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missed call to 9966044425 | Registered mobile, activated UAN, KYC linked | SMS with balance and last contribution | A quick check, no internet |
| SMS "EPFOHO UAN ENG" to 7738299899 | Same as above | SMS with balance and last contribution | A quick check, in your language |
| Passbook website | UAN and password | Full passbook for each employer, PDF download | Detailed records, interest check |
| UMANG app | UAN, mobile OTP | Passbook and balance on your phone | Mobile users; activating a UAN |

Both numbers are printed on [EPFO's passbook login page](https://passbook.epfindia.gov.in/MemberPassBook/login), along with the helpline, 1800118005.

## Method 1: missed call

1. From your registered mobile, call **9966044425**.
2. The call disconnects on its own after a couple of rings. It's free.
3. You'll get an SMS with your EPF balance and the latest contribution.

## Method 2: SMS

1. From your registered mobile, type **EPFOHO UAN ENG** and send it to **7738299899**. On EPFO's own page the format is written as "EPFOHO UAN <LAN>", with only the language code marked as something to fill in, so type "UAN" as it is. EPFO identifies you from your registered number.
2. Replace **ENG** with another language code if you prefer. ClearTax lists HIN (Hindi), TAM (Tamil), TEL (Telugu), KAN (Kannada), MAL (Malayalam), MAR (Marathi), GUJ (Gujarati), PUN (Punjabi) and BEN (Bengali).
3. The reply shows your balance and last contribution.

## Method 3: the passbook website (and PDF download)

This is the only method that shows the full history, and the one to use when checking whether interest has been credited.

1. Open **passbook.epfindia.gov.in**, or go to epfo.gov.in and choose **View Passbook**.
2. Enter your UAN, password and the captcha, and sign in.
3. The dashboard shows your total balance. If you've worked at more than one company, it lists a balance for each **member ID**, one per employer.
4. Choose **Passbook**, then select the member ID you want.
5. Scroll to see each month's employee share, employer share and pension contribution, plus withdrawals and the interest line.
6. To save it, choose **Download as PDF**.

Forgot the password? Use the password-reset option on EPFO's member portal, which works through your registered mobile.

## Method 4: UMANG app

1. Install UMANG (from the Play Store or App Store) and open the EPFO section.
2. Choose the passbook option under employee services. ClearTax gives the path as **EPFO > Employee Centric Services > View Passbook**.
3. Enter your UAN, verify with the OTP and select the member ID.

UMANG is also the easiest way to activate a UAN if you've never logged in, using face authentication on your phone.

## Reading the passbook

- **Employee share and employer share.** Your own contribution and your employer's contribution are shown in separate columns.
- **Pension contribution.** The passbook also shows contributions to the Employees' Pension Scheme in their own column, separate from the EPF balance.
- **Interest.** EPF interest is credited once a year, after the government ratifies the rate. For 2025–26, the rate is [8.25%](https://www.psuconnect.in/news/government-ratifies-8-25-epf-interest-rate-for-fy-2025-26-interest-likely-to-be-credited-to-over-7-crore-members-this-month). The board recommended it in March 2026 and the government ratified it on 19 June 2026, with crediting starting from June and possibly running into September. If your passbook doesn't show it yet, it may simply not have reached your account.
- **One member ID per employer.** Each past employer appears as a separate member ID, so you can check whether an old balance was ever transferred.

After the mid-2026 migration, some members found that the passbook showed only 2025–26, with a notice that earlier years were being migrated, as a Tamil explainer by Gen Infopedia showed in July. If older years are missing, check again later before raising a complaint.

## When it doesn't work

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No SMS after a missed call or text | Number not registered, or KYC not linked | Log in to the member portal and check your mobile number and KYC status |
| "Invalid UAN" or can't log in | UAN not activated | Activate it on the member portal or in UMANG |
| Passbook missing an employer | That member ID isn't linked to your UAN | Ask HR to confirm your UAN; link or transfer the old account on the member portal |
| Balance looks lower than expected | Contributions not yet deposited, or interest not yet credited | Check the latest month in the passbook; ask HR if months are missing |
| Nothing shows at all | Your employer may run an exempted PF trust | Ask HR for the trust's statement |

EPFO also warns on its passbook page that it never calls members asking for Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, an OTP or a payment. A caller offering to "release" your PF for a fee is a scam; our guide to [how UPI frauds work](https://techpulzo.in/how-upi-fraud-actually-happens-the-five-warning-signs) covers the same pattern, and these [phone settings cut down spam calls and SMS](https://techpulzo.in/how-to-stop-spam-calls-and-sms-on-an-indian-sim-the-settings-that-actually-help).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I check my EPF balance without a UAN?

Not directly. The SMS, missed-call, website and app services all depend on your UAN. If you don't know it, it's printed on most salary slips, and HR can give it to you. Once you have it, activate it once on the member portal or in UMANG, and every method above will work.

### Why haven't I received the 2025–26 interest yet?

The 8.25% rate for 2025–26 was ratified on 19 June 2026, and EPFO credits it to members' accounts over the following months; reports suggested the process could run into September. If your passbook still shows no interest entry for 2025–26, check again after some weeks, and confirm your account had no pending transfers or KYC problems.

### Does the missed call service cost anything?

No. The call disconnects automatically after a couple of rings, so you aren't charged, and the SMS reply is free. It only works from the mobile number registered with your UAN.

### How do I download my old employer's EPF passbook?

Log in to the passbook site, choose Passbook and select the old employer's member ID from the list; you can view and download it like the current one. If the old ID doesn't appear, it isn't linked to your UAN yet, and you'll need to link it or transfer that balance through the member portal.

### Is the balance in the SMS the same as the passbook?

Both come from EPFO's records. The SMS gives a summary: the balance and the latest contribution. The passbook breaks it down by month, employer and employee shares, plus interest. If the figures look different, use the passbook to see exactly which entries make up the balance, and contact the EPFO helpline (1800118005) if something doesn't add up.

## Check it once a quarter

A missed call takes ten seconds; opening the passbook takes two minutes. Doing one of them every few months catches the problems that are painful to fix years later: an employer that stopped depositing, a missing interest credit, or an old account that was never transferred.
