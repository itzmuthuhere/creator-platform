# Sources: Health insurance portability

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [Master Circular on Health Insurance Business, 29 May 2024](https://irdai.gov.in/documents/37343/365525/%e0%a4%b8%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%b5%e0%a4%be%e0%a4%b8%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%a5%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%af+%e0%a4%ac%e0%a5%80%e0%a4%ae%e0%a4%be+%e0%a4%b5%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%af%e0%a4%b5%e0%a4%b8%e0%a4%be%e0%a4%af+%e0%a4%aa%e0%a4%b0+%e0%a4%ae%e0%a4%be%e0%a4%b8%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%9f%e0%a4%b0+%e0%a4%aa%e0%a4%b0%e0%a4%bf%e0%a4%aa%e0%a4%a4%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%b0+_+Master+Circular++on+Health++Insurance+Business++29052024.pdf/5e707a91-b5de-1ec1-cf18-b66273a6839d?t=1716962621002&version=1.0), IRDAI. Accessed 2026-10-03 (PDF read). Used for: para 11 (migration credits) and para 12 (portability: joint data transfer; 72 hours via IIB; decision within 5 days; credits to the extent of SI for NCB, specific waiting periods, PED waiting, moratorium); para 13 (60-month moratorium counting ported/migrated credits); 30-day free look.

## Reputable secondary

- [IRDAI Guidelines for Health Insurance Portability](https://www.bajajgeneralinsurance.com/blog/health-insurance-articles/irda-health-insurance-portability-guidelines.html), Bajaj General Insurance, 29 Jul 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: apply at least 45 days before renewal; the new insurer can accept or reject; fresh waiting period only on enhanced SI; documents; premium per new underwriting; group to individual may lose features.
- [Health Insurance Portability Rules by IRDAI](https://joinditto.in/articles/health-insurance/health-insurance-portability-rules-by-irdai/), Ditto, updated 7 May 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 30–60 days (recommend 45–60); late applications risk a lapse; credits continue; tests possible.
