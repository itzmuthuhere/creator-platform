# Sources: How to check how many SIM cards are registered in your name

One line per source actually consulted. Only pages opened and read are listed.

## Primary / official

- [Know Mobile Connections in Your Name (TAFCOP)](https://tafcop.sancharsaathi.gov.in/telecomUser/) — Department of Telecommunications / C-DOT. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: login steps (mobile number, captcha, OTP), request counters (4,22,52,742 received; 3,98,56,550 resolved).
- [Sanchar Saathi home page and FAQ](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/Home/index.jsp) — Department of Telecommunications. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: the 9/6 connection limit, reporting steps and options, re-verification process and 30/45/60-day timelines, porting freeze, disconnection of excess connections, non-transferability and name change, FIR for forged documents, Chakshu scope, 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in referral.
- [Section 42, Telecommunications Act, 2023](https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/telecommunications/42.php) — bare act text. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: s.42(3)(e) obtaining SIMs through fraud, cheating or personation; up to 3 years, up to ₹50 lakh, or both.

## Reputable secondary

- [New SIM rules take effect across India; DoT caps mobile connections at 9 per person](https://www.freepressjournal.in/tech/new-sim-rules-take-effect-across-india-dot-caps-mobile-connections-at-9-per-person-orders-real-time-checks-by-november-2026) — Free Press Journal, 25 Aug 2026. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: DoT directive (23 Aug 2026), suspension of connections beyond the limit, real-time checks by 30 Nov 2026, Digital Intelligence Platform.
