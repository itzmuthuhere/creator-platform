# How to apply for a passport online in India: 2026 fees and steps

Applying for a passport online takes about half an hour on the Passport Seva portal, followed by one visit to a Passport Seva Kendra and a police verification at home. What changed in 2026 is the price: from 1 July 2026, a standard adult passport costs ₹2,500, up from ₹1,500, the first fee revision since 2012. New passports are also increasingly issued as chip-based e-passports.

This guide covers the new fees, the documents to keep ready, each step of the application, and what happens after your appointment.

## What changed recently

- **Fees went up on 1 July 2026.** The [Passport Seva website](https://services1.passportindia.gov.in/psp/FaqEPassport) carries the notice, and the full schedule is below.
- **The portal was rebuilt.** The Passport Seva Programme 2.0 went live across passport offices in May 2025. The Our Tech Tamil [walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uDZmK-97TwI) notes that logging in is now simpler, with OTPs sent by email, and there's a Document Advisor that tells you which papers you need.
- **E-passports.** Centres that have started issuing chip-based e-passports give you one automatically; there's no separate application. [Aditya Birla Capital's guide](https://www.adityabirlacapital.com/abc-of-money/passport-application-process-india) notes that existing passports stay valid until they expire, so there's no need to replace yours early.

## Passport fees from 1 July 2026

| Passport | Normal | Tatkaal |
|---|---|---|
| Adult, 36 pages (fresh or reissue) | ₹2,500 (was ₹1,500) | ₹5,000 |
| Adult, 60 pages | ₹3,500 (was ₹2,000) | ₹6,000 |
| Minor, 36 pages | ₹1,750 (was ₹1,000) | ₹4,250 |
| Adult, lost or damaged, 36 pages | ₹5,000 | ₹7,500 |
| Police Clearance Certificate | ₹750 | — |

*Source: [Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/india/passport-fees-hiked-from-july-1-36-page-booklet-now-rs-2500-and-tatkal-rs-5000-2026-06-25-129903), reporting the Passports (Amendment) Rules, 2026.*

A minor's passport (for children under 15) is valid for five years or until the child turns 18, whichever comes first, and children below 8 get a 10% discount on fresh applications, as [India.com reports](https://www.india.com/news/india/mea-amends-passports-rules-2026-check-validity-terms-schedule-iv-fee-guidelines-for-minors-8526912/).

**Example: a family of four** (two adults, a 6-year-old and a 12-year-old, all normal 36-page passports) now pays ₹2,500 + ₹2,500 + ₹1,575 + ₹1,750 = **₹8,325**, against ₹4,900 under the old fees.

Two choices affect the price:

- **36 or 60 pages?** The 60-page booklet costs ₹1,000 more. It's worth it only if you travel often to countries that stamp full pages or issue sticker visas; most people don't fill 36 pages in ten years.
- **Normal or Tatkaal?** Tatkaal doubles the adult fee to ₹5,000 for faster processing. Use it when you have a firm travel date you can't otherwise meet, not by default.

## Before you start: documents

The portal's Document Advisor gives the exact list for your case. Most adults need:

- **Proof of address:** Aadhaar is the usual choice; utility bills or a rent agreement are alternatives.
- **Proof of date of birth:** birth certificate, Class 10 certificate, PAN card or Aadhaar, depending on what the advisor accepts for you.
- **Your old passport** if you're renewing.
- **Educational certificates** if you're claiming Non-ECR status (Class 10 pass or above).

Make sure your name, date of birth and address match across these documents. Mismatches are the most common reason for being asked to return. If your Aadhaar address is out of date, update it first. The videos' advice to carry all your originals, including Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID and mark sheets, to the appointment is sound even if you won't need every one.

## Step by step

1. **Register.** On the Passport Seva portal, choose your passport office (by where you live; Tamil Nadu, for example, has regional offices in Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai and Tiruchirappalli, as Cosmopolitan TN's [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C4f3e4RZJDo) explains), enter your name and email, create a login ID and password, and verify with the OTP sent to your email.
2. **Start a fresh or reissue application.** Select normal or Tatkaal, and 36 or 60 pages.
3. **Fill in the nine sections:** passport type, applicant details, family details, present and permanent address, emergency contact, previous passport, other details, then review and verification.
4. **Take care with the address section.** Enter your PIN code and choose the **correct police station** from the list. Police verification goes to the station you pick, and a wrong choice delays it.
5. **Review everything** against your documents before submitting. Some errors can be corrected at the Kendra, but it's easier not to make them.
6. **Pay the fee and book an appointment** at a Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra (POPSK). The portal shows available dates for each centre; checking a few nearby centres can bring your date forward.
7. **Download or note the appointment receipt** with your application reference number.

Use a strong, unique password for the portal account, since it holds your identity details; a [password manager](https://techpulzo.in/how-to-set-up-a-password-manager-in-15-minutes) makes that easy.

## At the Passport Seva Kendra

Arrive a little before your slot with your originals and the appointment receipt. The visit usually has three counters:

1. **Token and document check.**
2. **Data entry, photo and fingerprints**, taken on the spot, so you don't bring photographs.
3. **Verification and approval**, where an official checks your documents and the application.

At the end you get an acknowledgement saying whether your file has been cleared, put on hold for something missing, or needs more documents.

## Police verification and delivery

For most fresh applications, a police officer from the station you selected visits your address to verify your identity and residence. Be available, keep your documents handy, and make sure neighbours or family can confirm you live there. Your Passport Verification Record can now be [stored in DigiLocker](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/meity-mea-launch-paperless-passport-verification-via-digilocker/) once it's issued.

After verification, the passport is printed and sent by Speed Post to your address. Track the status on the portal with your file number.

## Tatkaal: when it's worth it

Tatkaal is for when time matters more than ₹2,500: a confirmed trip, a job abroad, a family emergency. It moves your file faster, but it doesn't skip the document checks, so incomplete papers will still hold it up. If you have a few weeks, apply normally and book the earliest appointment you can find.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the passport fee in 2026?

From 1 July 2026, a normal 36-page adult passport costs ₹2,500 and Tatkaal costs ₹5,000. A 60-page passport is ₹3,500 (₹6,000 Tatkaal), and a minor's 36-page passport is ₹1,750 (₹4,250 Tatkaal).

### Do I need to replace my passport with an e-passport?

No. Existing passports remain valid until they expire. When you next apply or renew at a centre that issues e-passports, you'll receive the chip-based version automatically.

### Is Aadhaar mandatory for a passport application?

Aadhaar is the most common proof of address and identity on the form, and the portal asks for it, but the Document Advisor lists alternatives for your situation. Make sure the details on Aadhaar match your other documents, or update them first.

### Can I correct a mistake after submitting the online form?

Some errors can be fixed during your Passport Seva Kendra visit, but it's safer to review every field against your documents before you pay. Name and date-of-birth mismatches cause the most delays.

### How do I choose the right police station on the form?

After entering your PIN code, the form lists the police stations for your area. Pick the one that covers your exact address, because the verification request goes to that station. If you're unsure, ask at your local station before applying.

## The short checklist

Check the new fee, match your documents, pick the right police station, and book the earliest appointment across nearby centres. Do those four things and most applications go through without a second visit.
