# Research notes: How to apply for a passport online in India

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** Passport Apply Online 2026 | New Method A to Z (Our Tech Tamil), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uDZmK-97TwI. ~1 month old; auto-translated captions. New fee ₹2,500 (was ₹1,500), Tatkaal ₹5,000, under-15 ₹1,750; fee calculator; appointment availability check by PSK; revamped portal with email OTP login; Document Advisor; 9 form steps (passport type, applicant, family, address, emergency contact, previous passport, other details, review, verification); Aadhaar mandatory field; Non-ECR auto-set for graduates; choose correct police station via PIN; carry originals (Aadhaar, voter ID, PAN, mark sheets). Claims mistakes can be corrected at the PSK (partly true; still check).
- **Second:** How to Apply for a Passport in Tamil 2026 (Cosmopolitan TN), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C4f3e4RZJDo. ~2 months old, Tamil. Registration; TN has four RPOs (Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Trichy) and which districts map where; login ID; password rules.
- **Third:** Passport Apply Online 2026 (Sarkari DNA), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lNFVU5LvdoI. 2 weeks old, Hindi. Full form walkthrough.
