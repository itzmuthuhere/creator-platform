# Sources: Home loan prepayment, reduce EMI or tenure

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.
Format: `- [Page title](url) — Publisher. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD. Used for: what it supported.`

## Primary / official

- [Home Loans – Levy of fore-closure charges/pre-payment penalty](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/English/scripts/Notification.aspx?Id=986) — RBI, 5 Jun 2012 (DBOD.No.Dir.BC.107/13.03.00/2011-12). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: banks may not charge foreclosure or prepayment penalties on floating-rate home loans.
- [Reserve Bank of India (Pre-payment Charges on Loans) Directions, 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12878&Mode=0) — RBI, 2 Jul 2025 (RBI/2025-26/64). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: no prepayment charges on non-business floating-rate loans to individuals; any source of funds; no lock-in; applies to loans sanctioned or renewed from 1 Jan 2026.
- [Reset of Floating Interest Rate on EMI based Personal Loans](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12529&Mode=0) — RBI, 18 Aug 2023 (RBI/2023-24/55), updated 1 Oct 2025. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: borrower's choice of higher EMI, longer tenure, a mix, or prepayment at reset; no negative amortisation; quarterly statements with EMIs left.
- [Home Loans Interest Rates (Current)](https://sbi.bank.in/web/interest-rates/interest-rates/loan-schemes-interest-rates/home-loans-interest-rates-current) — State Bank of India. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: home loan 7.25–8.55%, MaxGain 7.75–9.05%, EBLR 7.90%, effective 20 May 2026.
- [Other schemes available at SBI (MaxGain)](https://homeloans.sbi.bank.in/products/view/other-schemes-available-at-sbi) — SBI. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: MaxGain sanctioned as an overdraft with drawing power reducing monthly.
- [Home Loan FAQ](https://onlineapply.sbi.bank.in/personal-banking/home-faq) — SBI. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: surplus can be parked in MaxGain and withdrawn; saves interest; linked to a savings/current account.
- [Part Prepayment Against Loan](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/myloans/part-prepayment-against-loan) — HDFC Bank. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: online part-prepayment request with a choice to reduce EMI or reduce tenure; charges, if any, shown before confirming.
- [Home Loans FAQs](https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/loans/home-loan/home-loans-faqs) — ICICI Bank. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: minimum part-prepayment of one EMI; an EMI once increased isn't decreased later except on part-prepayment or conversion.

## Reputable secondary

- [RBI MPC meet from Oct 5-7: Will RBI hike the repo rate this time?](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/news/rbi-mpc-meeting-october-2026-monetary-policy-repo-rate-hike-retail-inflation-126100200188_1.html) — Business Standard, 2 Oct 2026. - [Home Loan Tax Benefits Under Income Tax Act 2025](https://taxclue.in/blog/home-loan-tax-benefits-income-tax-act-2025) — TaxClue, updated 2 Oct 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: section mapping for FY 2026-27 (Section 123 principal within ₹1.5 lakh; no self-occupied interest deduction under Section 202 new regime).
