Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** Income Tax Rules 2026: Is the Old Regime More Attractive Now? (ET Money), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q22QzNEdMHM. 16:47, ~6 months old. 88% of individual taxpayers have moved to the new regime; Income-tax Rules 2026 (from 1 April 2026) raise several old-regime exemptions: 50% HRA for Ahmedabad, Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad; meal vouchers ₹200/meal; children education allowance ₹3,000/month/child; hostel ₹9,000; gifts ₹15,000; employer loans; higher car-lease perquisite values. Break-even deductions: ₹7.08 lakh at ₹20 lakh, ~₹8 lakh from ₹25 lakh. Example of a ₹36 lakh earner in Bengaluru saving ₹1.54 lakh under the old regime; caveats about salary structure, paperwork and HRA scrutiny (landlord relationship disclosure).
- **Second:** Old vs New Tax Regime 2026 (Unacademy), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-0Fnlbtfydw. ~6 months old. No slab changes for 2026-27; standard deduction ₹75,000 (new); zero tax up to ₹12 lakh (new) vs ₹5 lakh (old); deductions available in each.
