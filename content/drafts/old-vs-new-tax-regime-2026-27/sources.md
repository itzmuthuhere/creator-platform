## Primary / official

- [Salaried Individuals for AY 2026-27](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/individual/return-applicable-1) — Income Tax Department. Accessed 2026-10-02. ## Reputable secondary

- [India – Income-tax Rules 2026 (GMS Flash Alert 2026-081)](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-081.html) — KPMG, 30 Mar 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: rules from 1 April 2026; 50% HRA for Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune; meals ₹200; children education ₹3,000 and hostel ₹9,000 per child per month; gifts ₹15,000; medical-treatment loans ₹2 lakh; benefits apply under the old regime.
- [Income Tax Slabs FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27](https://cleartax.in/s/income-tax-slabs) — ClearTax. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: Budget 2026 made no change for 2026-27; standard deduction ₹75,000 (new) and ₹50,000 (old); marginal relief above ₹12 lakh; 4% cess; employer NPS in the new regime.
