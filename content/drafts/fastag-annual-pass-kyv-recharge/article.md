# FASTag annual pass, KYV and recharge: what car owners need to know in 2026

Three things changed for FASTag users in the past year. The FASTag annual pass, launched in August 2025, now costs ₹3,075 for 2026-27. NHAI dropped the routine Know Your Vehicle (KYV) check for cars from February 2026. And since November 2025, driving through a national highway toll without a working FASTag costs 1.25 times the toll if you pay by UPI, or double in cash.

This guide covers each change, works out whether the annual pass is worth it for the way you drive, and explains how to keep your tag working.

## The annual pass: the rules

The annual pass is a prepaid plan on your existing FASTag that covers toll at national highway plazas for a year. The rules, from NHAI's [user guide and FAQs](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/e-governance/digital-payment/fastag-annual-pass-user-guide-and-faqs?lgn=en):

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Price | ₹3,075 for 2026-27 (₹3,000 when launched on 15 August 2025) |
| Validity | One year from activation **or** 200 trips, whichever comes first |
| Vehicles | Private, non-commercial cars, jeeps and vans only, checked against the VAHAN registration database |
| Where it works | Fee plazas on National Highways and National Expressways |
| Where it doesn't | State highways, state-run expressways, local-body tolls and parking; your FASTag works normally there and deducts the toll |
| Your FASTag | Your existing tag works if it's fixed on the windshield, linked to your registration number, and not blacklisted |
| Not allowed | Tags registered only with a chassis number; transferring the pass to another vehicle; use on commercial vehicles |

The fee revision applies from 1 April 2026 and covers [more than a thousand fee plazas](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/nhai-revises-fastag-annual-pass-fee-to-%E2%82%B93075-for-fy-2026-27), according to News On AIR.

**What counts as a trip** matters, because 200 trips can go faster than you'd expect:

- At a normal (point-based) plaza, **each crossing is one trip**. A return journey through one plaza is two trips.
- On a closed-tolling expressway, where you pay by distance between entry and exit, **one entry and exit together is one trip**.

When the year ends or the 200 trips run out, the tag goes back to deducting toll from your balance until you buy another pass.

## Is the annual pass worth it?

The question is how many crossings you make a year and what the average toll is. If you use all 200 trips, the pass works out to about ₹15 a crossing. If you use fewer, it's worth it once your normal toll spending would exceed ₹3,075:

| Your average toll per crossing | Crossings a year to break even | What 200 crossings would cost without the pass |
|---|---|---|
| ₹50 | 62 | ₹10,000 |
| ₹80 | 38 | ₹16,000 |
| ₹100 | 31 | ₹20,000 |
| ₹150 | 20 | ₹30,000 |

*Our calculation, at ₹3,075 for the pass.*

Three common patterns:

- **Weekend trips out of the city.** Two plazas each way, twice a month, is 96 crossings a year. At ₹80 a crossing, that's ₹7,680 of toll against ₹3,075 for the pass. Clearly worth it.
- **Daily commuting through one plaza.** Two crossings a day, 22 working days a month, is 44 crossings a month, so the 200 trips run out in about four and a half months. You'd need two or three passes a year. That can still beat paying full toll, but check whether the plaza offers a cheaper monthly pass for local residents.
- **A few long trips a year.** If you cross 20 plazas a year, the pass rarely pays off unless the tolls are high. Pay as you go.

Remember that the pass doesn't cover state highways or state-run expressways, so the tolls you pay there don't count towards this sum.

## How to buy the annual pass

You can buy it only on the **Rajmargyatra app** or the **NHAI website**, not from your bank or a FASTag seller.

1. Open Rajmargyatra (or the NHAI site) and choose the annual pass option.
2. Enter your vehicle registration number and FASTag details. The system checks your vehicle and tag are eligible.
3. Pay ₹3,075.
4. The pass is normally active on your FASTag within two hours.

There's no new tag to fix. The moneycontrol [explainer video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zHfBGhJlrdQ) walks through these screens, though it shows the launch price of ₹3,000.

## KYV: when you still need it

Know Your Vehicle (KYV) was an extra check in which you uploaded photos of your car and tag to confirm the FASTag was on the right vehicle. Because it happened after the tag was already active, it often meant delays and repeated follow-ups. From 1 February 2026, [NHAI discontinued KYV for cars](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/kyv-discontinued-for-cars-on-new-fastag-issued-after-1-february-2026/) on new FASTags.

- **New tags:** the issuing bank must check your vehicle against the VAHAN database before activating the tag, or check your RC if VAHAN data isn't available. Tags bought online go through the same check, as [SCC Online's summary](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/01/03/nhai-fastag-know-your-vehicle-discontinued-2026/) explains. There's no follow-up check after activation.
- **Existing car tags:** no KYV is needed unless there's a complaint, such as a loose tag, a tag issued to the wrong vehicle, or misuse.

If you get a KYV message for a car tag with no complaint against it, check that it's genuinely from your issuing bank before sharing anything. Videos made before February 2026 that walk through KYV, like Ganga info's [guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K23Pc85wbPE), are now mostly relevant only when a bank asks for it.

## Recharging and keeping your tag working

Your FASTag is a prepaid account with the bank that issued it. You can recharge it through the bank's app or website, through UPI apps that list FASTag recharge, or through the bank's linked account if you've set up auto-recharge. Your bank sends an SMS for each toll deduction and recharge, which is the easiest way to check your balance.

To avoid problems at the plaza:

- **Keep enough balance before a trip.** A tag with too little balance, or one that's blacklisted, may not be accepted.
- **One vehicle, one tag.** If you sell a car or change its tag, close the old FASTag with the issuer.
- **Make sure the tag is fixed properly** on the inside of the windshield. A tag kept loose in the car can attract a complaint and a KYV request.
- **Check that it's linked to your registration number,** not only the chassis number, especially if you plan to buy the annual pass.

If you pay recharges by card, a card with cashback on bill payments or UPI can recover a little; our comparison of [credit and debit cards for everyday spending](https://techpulzo.in/credit-card-vs-debit-card-for-everyday-spending-what-actually-saves-you-money) covers how those rewards work.

## What you pay without a working FASTag

Since 15 November 2025, the rule for vehicles without a valid, working FASTag at national highway plazas is, as [Autocar India reported](https://www.autocarindia.com/car-news/new-fastag-rule-upi-payments-to-attract-25-percent-higher-fee-437602):

| Payment | What you pay on a ₹100 toll |
|---|---|
| Working FASTag | ₹100 |
| UPI at the plaza (no working FASTag) | ₹125 |
| Cash (no working FASTag) | ₹200 |

So if your tag fails, paying by UPI limits the damage. Better still, check your balance and tag status before you leave.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the FASTag annual pass price in 2026?

₹3,075 for the 2026-27 financial year, up from ₹3,000 when the pass launched in August 2025. It covers one year or 200 trips, whichever ends first, at national highway and national expressway fee plazas.

### Does the FASTag annual pass work on state highways?

No. It's valid only at fee plazas on National Highways and National Expressways. On state highways, state-run expressways, local tolls and parking lots, your FASTag works as normal and the toll is deducted from your balance.

### How are trips counted on the annual pass?

At a normal toll plaza, every crossing counts as one trip, so a return journey through one plaza uses two. On closed-tolling expressways, where toll depends on distance, one entry and exit together counts as a single trip.

### Do I need to do KYV for my FASTag in 2026?

Not for a car, in most cases. NHAI stopped routine KYV for cars on new FASTags from 1 February 2026, and existing car tags need it only if there's a complaint, such as a loose tag or misuse. New tags are verified against VAHAN before activation.

### What happens if I cross a toll without a working FASTag?

At national highway plazas, you pay 1.25 times the toll if you pay by UPI, or twice the toll in cash. This rule has applied since 15 November 2025.

## The one calculation to do

Count the national highway toll crossings you made last year and multiply by your usual toll. If that's more than ₹3,075, the annual pass saves you money; if you'd use up 200 trips in a few months, compare it with a local monthly pass first.
