Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Basic Gyaan, "What is Masked Aadhaar Card and How to Download it?"** (3:20, Hindi): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KSQzxyNxGT0. Masked hides the first 8 digits; framed against loan-app and KYC fraud; says it can't be used for government schemes; usable as ID and address proof; hotels and cyber cafés are common leak points; download via myAadhaar with the masked option; the password format.
- **Times Now Navbharat, "Masked Aadhaar Card क्या है?"** (4:25, Hindi): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0s5TVh8rCU. Where to use it (hotels, stations, airports, shops, strangers); download steps; aside on free email update via the app.
- **B Visitor, "where to use masked aadhaar / kaha valid nahi hai"** (3:04, Hindi): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fz_v2M1jeQ8. A viewer's masked Aadhaar was refused at an exam; the creator links this to DBT and government schemes needing the full number; says agencies must accept e-Aadhaar.
- A fourth video (JEEVA All Rounder, Tamil, on password errors) had unusable captions and was dropped.

## 2. Topic assessment

**Verdict:** PROCEED. High search interest, and UIDAI's FAQ is clear on the facts. The videos overstate what masking protects against and don't mention VID, the Aadhaar app's selective sharing, biometric lock, or the 2022 advisory saga.

**Angle:** what masking does and doesn't protect, how to get it, where to use it versus the full number, and the newer tools (VID, app sharing, biometric lock) that protect more than masking.
