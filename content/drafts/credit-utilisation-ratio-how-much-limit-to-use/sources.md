# Sources: Credit utilisation ratio

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [Six Ways You Can Use Your Credit Card For An Optimum Credit Score](https://www.cibil.com/blog/six-ways-you-can-use-credit-card-for-optimum-credit-score), TransUnion CIBIL. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 30% of the overall limit described as a "healthy ratio"; too many cards add to credit exposure; pay in full on time.

## Reputable secondary

- [RBI Mandates Fortnightly Credit Info Reporting by CICs and CIs](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-mandates-fortnightly-credit-info-reporting-cics-cis.html), TaxGuru. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: RBI/2024-25/60; fortnightly reporting as on the 15th and last day from 1 Jan 2025; submission within 7 days; CIC ingestion within 5 days.
- [Master Directions On Credit Card And Debit Card](https://www.mondaq.com/india/financial-services/1214312/master-directions-on-credit-card-and-debit-card-issuance-and-conduct-directions-2022), Mondaq, 22 Jul 2022. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: limit not breached without explicit consent; one-time billing cycle change; closure within 7 working days.

## Source videos

- [Now No Need To Maintain 30% Credit Card Utilization](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jXdKQY-sc_M), Utkarsh Ojha (YouTube, Hindi). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: a credit report showing ~12% overall while one card was ~53% used (overall ratio matters).
- [Credit card utilization ratio explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sJfwGvrOxS8), IT Masti (YouTube, Hindi). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 3 cards × ₹1 lakh → ₹90,000 at 30% across cards.
