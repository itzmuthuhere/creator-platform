# How to port your mobile number to another network in India

To port your mobile number to Jio, Airtel, Vi or BSNL and keep the same number, you send one SMS to get a porting code, take that code to the new operator, and your number switches over within a few working days. The process is set by TRAI, the telecom regulator, and is the same whichever operator you're leaving or joining. Most failures happen at the first step, when the code doesn't arrive because one of TRAI's conditions isn't met.

This guide covers those conditions, each step, how long porting takes, and what to sort out before your old SIM stops working.

## Before you start: can you port?

TRAI's [MNP FAQ](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-11/Modified%20MNP%20FAQ%2030.09.2024%20(2)_0.pdf) lists the conditions for getting a porting code. You'll be refused if any of these apply:

| Condition | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Postpaid bills unpaid | Clear all billed dues first |
| Less than 90 days on your current network | Wait until the number has been active there for 90 days |
| A change of ownership is in progress | Finish or cancel that first |
| Contract obligations pending | For example, an exit clause on a plan or device deal |
| A court has barred porting, or the number is sub-judice | Rare for individuals |
| SIM swapped or replaced in the last 7 days | Wait seven days after getting a replacement SIM |

The seven-day rule after a SIM swap was added from 1 July 2024 to stop fraudsters who get a duplicate SIM and then port the number away, as [TeamLease RegTech's summary](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/30631/telecommunication-mobile-number-portability-ninth-amendment-regulation/) of the amendment explains. If your request is refused, you get an SMS giving the reason.

## Step by step

1. **Get your porting code (UPC).** From the number you want to port, send an SMS to **1900** that reads:
   `PORT 9876543210` (the word PORT, a space, and your ten-digit number). You'll get an 8-digit **Unique Porting Code** by SMS from **1901**.
2. **Go to the new operator.** Visit a store, or use the operator's app or website if it offers porting online. IND Hindi Tech's [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQ8AGda21P0) shows Jio letting you start the process from home and delivering the SIM. Give your UPC, fill in the application and porting form, and complete KYC, usually with Aadhaar.
3. **Pay the porting charge if asked.** It can't be more than **₹6.46**.
4. **Collect the new SIM.** You'll get an SMS confirming your porting request, with a 24-hour window to withdraw it.
5. **Wait for the switchover SMS.** It tells you the date and time, which is at night.
6. **Insert the new SIM** when the old one loses signal, and your number is live on the new network.

Use the UPC quickly: it's valid for **4 days** (30 days in Jammu and Kashmir, Assam and the North East), not counting the day it's issued. If it expires, request a new one.

## How long porting takes

| Situation | Time |
|---|---|
| Within the same telecom circle (for example, within Tamil Nadu) | 3 working days |
| To a different circle (for example, Delhi to Mumbai) | 5 working days |
| Corporate numbers | 5 working days |
| Jammu and Kashmir, Assam and the North East | Up to 15 working days |

*Source: TRAI.*

The actual switch happens at night, and you may have no service for up to four hours. Plan for that: don't port the night before you need to receive an important OTP.

TRAI also says that once you've submitted a valid UPC within its validity period, an individual's porting request can't be rejected by your old operator.

## Why your UPC didn't arrive

- **No active plan or SMS pack.** The PORT message needs a valid recharge to send, as Mr Zaman's [troubleshooting video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O13liV79OV8) points out. Recharge with the smallest pack that includes SMS.
- **The message format is wrong.** It must be PORT, a space, then the ten-digit number, sent to 1900 from that same number.
- **One of TRAI's conditions isn't met.** Check the rejection SMS: unpaid postpaid dues, a SIM swap in the last seven days, or less than 90 days on the network are the usual reasons.
- **Network delay.** Wait a few minutes and try once more before assuming something's wrong.

## Changing your mind

You can cancel within **24 hours** of submitting the porting request by sending an SMS to 1900:
`CANCEL 9876543210`
This cancels the request and the UPC. Any porting charge you paid isn't refunded.

## What to sort out before the switch

- **Postpaid:** pay your last bill; a final bill may still come for the days before the switch.
- **Prepaid:** unused balance and plan validity don't move to the new operator. Time your port close to the end of your current plan.
- **OTPs and two-factor login:** your number stays the same, so bank, UPI and app OTPs continue on the new network. Just avoid the switchover night for anything urgent. Our guide to [two-factor authentication](https://techpulzo.in/how-two-factor-authentication-actually-stops-account-hacks) explains why the number matters.
- **Choose the plan before you port:** compare the new operator's prepaid and postpaid options first; our comparison of [prepaid vs postpaid costs](https://techpulzo.in/prepaid-vs-postpaid-mobile-plans-which-actually-costs-less-over-a-year) can help.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get a UPC code to port my number?

Send an SMS from the number you want to port to 1900 with the text PORT followed by a space and your ten-digit number. The 8-digit UPC arrives by SMS from 1901. You need an active plan that allows SMS.

### How long does mobile number porting take?

Three working days within the same telecom circle, and five working days between circles or for corporate numbers. In Jammu and Kashmir, Assam and the North East it can take up to 15 working days. The final switch happens at night with up to four hours without service.

### Why is my UPC request being rejected?

TRAI rejects a UPC request if postpaid dues are unpaid, the number has been on the current network for less than 90 days, a SIM swap or replacement happened in the last seven days, a change of ownership is in progress, contract obligations are pending, or porting is barred by a court. The rejection SMS states the reason.

### Is there a fee for porting a mobile number?

TRAI caps the charge at ₹6.46 per porting request, payable only if the new operator asks for it. You'll also pay for the new plan you choose.

### Can I cancel a porting request?

Yes, within 24 hours of submitting it. Send CANCEL followed by a space and your ten-digit number to 1900. The porting charge, if you paid one, isn't refunded.

## The quick version

Check the 90-day and SIM-swap rules, clear any postpaid dues, send PORT and your number to 1900, and take the code to your new operator within four days. Then expect a night-time switch about three working days later.
