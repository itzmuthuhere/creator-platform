# Research notes: Gold ETF vs digital gold vs physical gold

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** SEBI just warned investors about Digital Gold - Here's why (Markets by Zerodha, The Daily Brief #348), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=syW84KpuLCg. Nov 2025. Digital gold is outside SEBI's framework; your claim is a private contract with the platform (counterparty risk); unclear whether gold is segregated or allocated; 3% GST on purchase because legally it's a sale of goods; platform buy-sell spread about 2–3%; ₹10,000 becomes about ₹9,400–9,500 of gold immediately; gold ETFs sit in demat with trustees, custodians, daily NAV. Second half of the video is about coal gasification (ignored).
- **Second:** Don't Buy Gold Until You Watch This: Physical vs ETF vs SGB, ft. Alok Jain (Moneytalks by Groww), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-QVmJ-KpE0Q. ~3 months old, Hindi. Opinion-heavy: prefers physical gold because ETFs/MFs depend on the AMC (counterparty risk); rule of thumb "age ÷ 2" as % of net worth in gold; equity/gold/real estate split. OPINION, attributed and balanced.
- **Third:** How To Invest In Gold - Physical vs Digital vs Gold ETF vs Gold MF (XY-Axis Education), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e50lQ7YgbtA. ~8 months old, Hindi. Walks through each route live; jeweller making charges and GST; digital gold selling charges on apps; ETF vs gold mutual fund; SGB discontinued.
