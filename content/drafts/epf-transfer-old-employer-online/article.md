# EPF transfer from an old employer: how to move your PF online in 2026

Every time you change jobs, your new employer opens a fresh PF account (a "Member ID") under your Universal Account Number (UAN). The money in the old account doesn't move by itself in every case, and if you leave it there you lose more than tidiness: a shorter service record can mean tax on a later withdrawal, and scattered balances make every claim harder. An EPF transfer from an old employer pulls those balances into your current account. For most people in 2026 it's a ten-minute online request that no employer has to approve.

This guide covers when you need to do it, the checks to make first, the steps on EPFO's member portal, and what to do if the transfer stalls.

## Why transferring matters

When you open **Member Service History** on EPFO's portal, it now shows a pop-up listing the benefits of transferring. MY CHANNEL MY KNOWLEDGE's [walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EDgMmdTFuXg) reads them out: a larger amount available for advances and final settlement, avoiding TDS, a better pension, and insurance cover. The tax point is the one with real money attached.

**The five-year rule.** A PF withdrawal is tax-free only after five years of continuous service. [ClearTax's guide to EPF withdrawal tax](https://cleartax.in/s/pf-balance-withdrawal-incometax) explains that service with earlier employers counts towards those five years when the balance has been transferred to your current account. If you withdraw before five years and the amount is over ₹50,000, EPFO deducts TDS at 10% (20% without PAN), and the employer's share and interest are taxable in that year.

**Example.** Priya worked three years at her first company and has been two years at her second. Her old account holds ₹1.8 lakh.

| | Old account left alone | Old account transferred |
|---|---|---|
| Service counted for the tax rule | 3 years at the first company, 2 at the second | 5 years combined |
| If she settles the old account now | TDS of about ₹18,000 (10%), and part of it taxable | Not taxable once combined service reaches five years |
| Pension service | Split across two Member IDs | Joined on one record |

The numbers are illustrative; your own taxable amount depends on how much was employer contribution and interest.

## Do you even need to apply?

Some transfers now happen on their own. EPFO says about 34.5% of the 1.30 crore online transfer claims it received between April 2024 and January 2025 were **auto-generated**, according to its [January 2025 press release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2094230). So check first:

1. Log in to the EPFO member portal (unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in) with your UAN, password and OTP.
2. Open **Online Services > Member Service History**.
3. The top of the page shows your **Primary Member ID** (your current job). Below, older accounts that still hold money are marked **Due for transfer**.

If nothing is listed as due, your old balances have already moved. If something is, read on.

## Who needs employer approval

Since January 2025 most transfer requests go straight to EPFO. The same press release says the change was expected to route about 94% of transfer claims to EPFO without the employer. [India TV's report](https://www.indiatvnews.com/business/personal-finance/epfo-pf-account-transfer-online-aadhaar-uan-e-sewa-portal-2025-01-19-972061) gives the conditions:

- **Your UAN was issued on or after 1 October 2017 and is linked to Aadhaar:** no employer step.
- **You have two UANs, both from after that date and linked to the same Aadhaar:** no employer step.
- **Your UAN is older than October 2017:** no employer step if it's Aadhaar-linked and your name, date of birth and gender match across the accounts. Otherwise the claim goes to an employer to attest.

A second change in April 2025 removed approval at the receiving EPF office. In [PIB's words](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2124244), once the old office approves the claim, the balance moves to your current account "instantly", with the taxable and non-taxable parts recorded separately so TDS is worked out correctly later.

## Before you apply: three checks

1. **Date of exit on the old account.** The transfer needs a date of exit against the old Member ID. If the Service History page shows it blank, add it yourself under **Manage > Mark Exit** with an Aadhaar OTP. [Stable Money's guide](https://stablemoney.in/blog/epf-exit-date-update-online) notes that EPFO allows this only two months after the last contribution from that employer.
2. **Aadhaar linked and details matching.** Your name, date of birth and gender should match Aadhaar on every account; mismatches are the usual reason a claim is sent back or routed to an employer. Members with Aadhaar-verified UANs can correct most details themselves.
3. **A current employer account.** You need an active Member ID with your present employer, since that is where the money goes.

The Online PF Consultant [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YJRbEZ0Wdws) points out that bank and PAN details don't affect a transfer, because the money moves between PF accounts and never reaches a bank. They still matter later for withdrawals.

## How to transfer PF online, step by step

1. On the member portal, go to **Online Services > Request for Transfer of Account**. (The Service History page also has a link to the same form.)
2. Check the personal details and current employer shown at the top.
3. Under **Step 1**, find your old account. Enter your **UAN** and tap **Get Details**; this lists every Member ID due for transfer, which is easier than typing an old Member ID.
4. Select the account or accounts to move. You can request several at once.
5. Under **Step 2**, tap **Get OTP**. The OTP goes to your Aadhaar-linked mobile. Enter it and **Submit**.
6. Note the claim reference. If an employer has to attest your claim, the portal tells you which one.

## Tracking it

After the portal upgrade, EPFO runs two tracking pages. The MY CHANNEL MY KNOWLEDGE video explains that claims filed before the June 2026 shutdown use **Track Claim Status**, while those filed after services resumed on 3 July 2026 use the **new** Track Claim Status page, which has a separate line for transfer claims. [Kustodian's summary](https://kustodian.life/provident-fund/epfo-latest-news/epfo-portal-update-july-2026) of the upgrade also notes that EPFO warned of extra verification, and slower processing, while the new system settles.

When it's done, the old Member ID shows a nil balance and your current passbook shows the transferred PF and pension amounts. Check both.

## If the transfer gets stuck

- **"Date of exit not available":** mark the exit yourself (see above). If it's been less than two months since the last contribution, wait.
- **Pending with employer:** your UAN is pre-2017 or details don't match. Ask the old or current employer's HR to approve it on their portal; if the old company has shut, the current employer can attest.
- **Rejected for mismatched details:** correct the name, date of birth or gender to match Aadhaar, then file again.
- **No movement after several weeks:** raise a complaint on EPFiGMS, EPFO's grievance portal, with the claim reference.

Don't pay agents or share your OTP to "speed up" a transfer. EPFO's online transfer is free, and the same scam patterns appear in our guide to [how UPI fraud happens](https://techpulzo.in/how-upi-fraud-actually-happens-the-five-warning-signs).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need my old employer's approval to transfer PF?

Usually not. Since January 2025, claims go directly to EPFO when your UAN was issued from October 2017 onwards and is Aadhaar-linked, or when an older UAN is Aadhaar-linked and your name, date of birth and gender match. Other cases still need an employer to attest.

### How long does an EPF transfer take?

There's no fixed time. Since April 2025, the balance is credited as soon as the old EPF office approves the claim, but approval itself can take days or weeks, and EPFO warned of extra checks after its July 2026 portal upgrade.

### Should I transfer or withdraw my old PF?

Transfer, unless you genuinely need the money. Withdrawing before five years of continuous service makes much of it taxable with 10% TDS above ₹50,000, while transferring keeps your service continuous and the balance earning interest.

### Can I transfer more than one old PF account at once?

Yes. Enter your UAN on the transfer form, tap Get Details, and select every account listed as due for transfer before submitting with the OTP.

### What if my old company has closed?

The claim can be attested by your current employer if one is needed. If your UAN is from October 2017 or later and Aadhaar-linked, no employer is involved at all.

## The short version

Open Member Service History, see what's due for transfer, fill in any missing date of exit, then use Request for Transfer of Account with your UAN and an Aadhaar OTP. Doing it each time you change jobs keeps your service continuous, which is what keeps a later withdrawal tax-free.
