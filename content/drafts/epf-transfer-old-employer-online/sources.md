# Sources: EPF transfer from an old employer

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [EPFO Simplifies PF transfer Process to Enhance Service Delivery](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2094230), PIB (Ministry of Labour & Employment), 19 Jan 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: employer routing removed in most cases; 94% (1.20 of 1.30 crore) estimate; 34.5% auto-generated; 17% of grievances.
- [EPFO Simplifies Transfer Claim Process Through Revamped Form 13 Functionality](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2124244), PIB, 25 Apr 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: destination office approval removed; instant credit after source office approval; taxable/non-taxable split; 1.25 crore members, ₹90,000 crore.

## Reputable secondary

- [EPFO simplifies rules: Employees can now transfer PF accounts themselves](https://www.indiatvnews.com/business/personal-finance/epfo-pf-account-transfer-online-aadhaar-uan-e-sewa-portal-2025-01-19-972061), India TV, 19 Jan 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the three scenarios with no employer intervention.
- [PF transfer made easy: EPFO's revamped Form 13](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/latest-updates/pf-transfer-made-easy-all-you-need-to-know-about-epfo-s-revamped-form-13/article-171537/), Upstox, 3 Jun 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: corroborating the Form 13 revamp.
- [Employer's approval not required: EPFO simplifies PF account transfer process](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/employers-approval-not-required-epfo-simplifies-pf-account-transfer-process-on-job-change), The Tribune (PTI), 25 Apr 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: corroborating the April 2025 change.
- [EPFO Portal Update 2026](https://kustodian.life/provident-fund/epfo-latest-news/epfo-portal-update-july-2026), Kustodian, updated 3 Jul 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 26 Jun–3 Jul 2026 outage; redesigned Member Service History; old and new Track Claim Status pages; extra verification after migration.
- [Income Tax on EPF Withdrawal](https://cleartax.in/s/pf-balance-withdrawal-incometax), ClearTax. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 5-year rule including transferred previous service; TDS 10%/20% above ₹50,000; taxable components.
- [How to Transfer PF Online Using UAN](https://cleartax.in/s/online-epf-transfer-procedure), ClearTax. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: attestation by the previous or current employer when needed (its Form 13 printing steps are outdated and were not used).
- [EPF exit date update online](https://stablemoney.in/blog/epf-exit-date-update-online), Stable Money. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the Mark Exit path; the two-month rule; the OTP.
