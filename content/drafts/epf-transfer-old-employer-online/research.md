# Research notes: EPF transfer from an old employer

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** PF Transfer New Process 2026 (MY CHANNEL MY KNOWLEDGE), EDgMmdTFuXg. About 2 months old, English auto-captions. Shows the process after 3 July 2026:
  - Log in, then open Member Service History. A pop-up lists four benefits: a higher withdrawal, avoiding TDS, a higher pension, and EDLI cover up to ₹7 lakh.
  - Check the "Due for transfer" list; the date of exit must be present.
  - Online Services > Request for Transfer of Account; enter the UAN > Get Details; select the IDs; confirm with an Aadhaar OTP.
  - Two Track Claim Status pages: old (before 26 Jun 2026) and new (after 3 Jul 2026).
- **Second:** Online PF Consultant, YJRbEZ0Wdws. 4 days old, Hindi.
  - No employer needed for jobs after Oct 2017.
  - The PF office takes "2–3 days" (the consultant's claim; not used).
  - Bank and PAN are irrelevant for a transfer; the exit date is the key; all companies can be done together.
  - Promotes paid services (ignored).
- **Third:** MySimpleGuide, XcF7JD9Dp4k, Hindi. Basic flow with no employer.
