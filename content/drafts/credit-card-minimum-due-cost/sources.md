# Sources: What paying only the credit card minimum due costs you

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.
Format: `- [Page title](url) — Publisher. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD. Used for: what it supported.`

## Primary / official

- [Master Direction – Credit Card and Debit Card – Issuance and Conduct Directions, 2022](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12300) — RBI, updated 7 Mar 2024. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: no negative amortisation and no capitalising unpaid charges (9(b)(ii)); mandatory minimum-payment warning and suspension of the interest-free period (9(b)(iii), 3(xvi)); past-due reporting and penal charges only after more than three days, late fee only on the amount outstanding after the due date (9(b)(v)); APR disclosure (9(b)(i)).
- [Most Important Terms & Conditions (credit cards), Version 4.12](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/content/dam/hdfcbankpws/in/en/personal-banking/discover-products/cards/credit-cards/personal-mitc/mitc-in-english.pdf) — HDFC Bank, Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: 3.75%/month (45% a year) finance charge for most cards, 1.99% for premium variants; interest on all transactions from the transaction date when the balance isn't paid in full; new transactions charged until previous dues are cleared; GST at 18% on interest; interest-free period of 20–50 days, lost when a previous balance is unpaid; MAD formula from June 2025 (rounded up; no fixed rupee floor stated).
- [Most Important Terms & Conditions](https://www.sbicard.com/sbi-card-en/assets/docs/pdf/most-important-terms-and-conditions/mitc-english.pdf) — SBI Card. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: finance charges up to 3.75%/month (45% a year) from the transaction date; MAD = 100% of GST, EMIs, fees, finance charges, overlimit + 2% of the remaining balance (worked example); interest-free period suspended after a minimum-only payment; late payment charges ₹100–₹1,300; NPA if MAD unpaid for 90 days.
- [SmartEMI](https://www.hdfc.bank.in/smartemi) — HDFC Bank. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: converting card outstanding to EMI over 6–48 months; transactions up to 60 days old; Q1 2026-27 IRR 10.80–24.96% (average 18.20%), APR 21.57%.
- [The cost of anchoring on credit-card minimum repayments](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/2552/) — Neil Stewart, Psychological Science 20(1), 2009 (Warwick repository record and abstract). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: minimum-payment information acts as an anchor that lowers repayments.
