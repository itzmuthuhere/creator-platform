# Research notes: How to link PAN with Aadhaar and check the status

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Sarkari DNA, "Aadhar PAN Card Link 2026 | Inoperative PAN Fix"** (9:21, Hindi auto): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YSvNdbGWdPE. Full fee-and-link walkthrough: check status first (PANs made through Aadhaar are often already linked), "payment details not found", e-Pay Tax, sub-type "fee for delay in linking PAN with Aadhaar", ₹1,000, any bank or UPI works, the payment alone doesn't link, wait for the payment to reflect (he says 2–2.5 hours), then submit the link request; name as on Aadhaar; "I have only year of birth" box only for old Aadhaar cards.
- **CA Sumit Sharma, "PAN-Aadhar Link Status Check"** (3:34, Hindi): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hM6T8V-lIE. Status check without login; consequences in brief (higher TDS, no refund); if linked to someone else's Aadhaar, raise a grievance.
- **CA Monica, "PAN Inoperative & Higher TDS"** (5:42, Tamil): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ojOGno01l3U. Circular 9/2025: relief from the higher-TDS default if the PAN becomes operative within two months of the month-end of the transaction; the deductor then files a TDS correction.
- **Easy Financial Express** (3:32, Tamil): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R47xeTh40dI. Name mismatch causes errors; PAN-Aadhaar linking isn't the same as bank KYC.
- **Tech and Technics, "Pan Aadhaar Link Online After 1st April 2026"** (9:56, Tamil): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M43LbzYHLRU. Payment walkthrough for tax year 2026-27; check that name, DOB, gender (and father's name) match before paying, or the ₹1,000 is wasted.

## 2. Topic assessment

PROCEED. High search volume, official sources are good, and the rules changed in 2025–26 (new Income-tax Act from April 2026, Circular 9/2025, the Aadhaar Enrolment ID deadline), which the videos cover only partly and sometimes wrongly (waiting time).

**Verdict:** PROCEED. **Angle:** the order that avoids wasting ₹1,000 (status → match details → pay → wait → link → confirm), what "inoperative" does to you in practice, and the TDS-relief window.
