# Sources: How to link PAN with Aadhaar and check the status

One line per source actually consulted. Only pages opened and read are listed.

## Primary / official

- [Link Aadhaar: help page](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/link-aadhaar) — Income Tax Department. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: who must link, exemptions, ₹1,000 fee, consequences of an inoperative PAN, Notification 26/2025 (Aadhaar Enrolment ID PANs, 31 Dec 2025 / 1 Jan 2026).
- [Link Aadhaar: user manual](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/link-aadhaar-um) — Income Tax Department (last updated 29 Sep 2026). Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: s.139AA / s.262 of the Income-tax Act 2025, PANs allotted on or before 1 July 2017, fee payment steps (Major head 0021, Minor head 500, sub-type), 4–5 working-day wait, pre- and post-login linking, status check, JAO delinking, UIDAI pending.
- [e-Filing portal home](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/) — Income Tax Department. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: Quick Links (Link Aadhaar/View Status, Know Your JAO), integrated payment module note.

- [Protean online PAN services: application types](https://www.onlineservices.proteantech.in/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html) — Protean eGov Technologies (authorised by the Income Tax Department). Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: "Changes or Correction in existing PAN Data" application; name as per Aadhaar printed on PAN.
- [Updating data on Aadhaar](https://uidai.gov.in/en/updating-data-on-aadhaar) — UIDAI. Accessed 2026-09-29. Used for: demographic updates including DoB/gender corrections for enrolment errors.
