# Sources: Direct vs regular mutual funds

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.
Format: `- [Page title](url) — Publisher. Accessed YYYY-MM-DD. Used for: what it supported.`

## Primary / official

- [Circular CIR/IMD/DF/21/2012: Steps to re-energize the Mutual Fund industry](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/1347547815927.pdf) — SEBI, 13 Sep 2012. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: direct plans must exist in every scheme, have a lower expense ratio excluding distribution expenses and commission, pay no commission, and have a separate NAV (para D).
- [PR No. 84/2025: SEBI Board Meeting (212th)](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/dec-2025/1765977060104.pdf) — SEBI, Dec 2025. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: Base Expense Ratio replaces the old limit and excludes statutory levies; full slab table (2.10% up to ₹500 cr … 0.95% above ₹50,000 cr for equity schemes; 0.90% for index funds/ETFs); TER = BER + brokerage + levies; removal of the extra 5 bps.
- [TER of MF Schemes](https://www.amfiindia.com/ter-of-mf-schemes) — AMFI. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: per-scheme regular and direct BER/TER data (pulled for September 2026 via the page's own data API, script in `scripts/research/amfi-ter-gap.mjs`); the note that direct plans' BER excludes distribution expenses and commission; Regulation 66(7) of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026.
- [Addendum: Discontinuation of levying exit load for switch of investments from Regular to Direct Plan](https://files.hdfcfund.com/s3fs-public/2025-04/1294-%20Addendum-%20Discontinuation%20of%20levying%20exit%20load%20for%20switch%20of%20investments%20from%20Regular%20to%20Direct%20Plan.pdf) — HDFC Mutual Fund, 17 Apr 2025. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: AMFI's 9 Apr 2025 instruction; no exit load on regular→direct switches in the same scheme from 21 Apr 2025; exit load still applies on later redemption within the load period from the original purchase date.
- [Consolidated Account Statement (CAS)](https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/become-mf-distributor?zoneName=consolidatedAcct) — AMFI. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: who issues the CAS (NSDL/CDSL, or the fund when PAN isn't in demat) and that it arrives by the 10th of the following month.

## Reputable secondary

- [SEBI's new expense ratio framework: Here is what changes after April 1, 2026](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/37012-sebis-new-expense-ratio-framework-here-is-what-changes-after-april-1-2026) — Cafemutual, 17 Feb 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: effective date of 1 April 2026; what BER includes (management fee, distribution commission, RTA, custodian, audit, marketing) and excludes.
- [Retail investors power direct mutual fund shift as digital access grows](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/mutual-fund/markets-mutual-fund-retail-investors-direct-plan-mutual-fund-aum-growth-126090600173_1.html) — Business Standard, 7 Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: AMFI–CRISIL data, direct plans 45.1% of industry AUM and 36.7% of retail AUM in March 2026 (21.4% in March 2021).
- [Direct plans cost less. Switching to them might not be free](https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stories/228961/switch-regular-to-direct-mutual-fund-plan-tax-cost-how/) — Value Research, 2 Jul 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: switch counts as a sale; exit load no longer applies; four switch routes (AMC site, myCAMS/KFinKart, MF Central, platforms); start the new SIP before cancelling the old one; international funds closed to fresh money can block a switch.
- [LTCG Tax on Mutual Funds 2026](https://www.bajajamc.com/knowledge-centre/common-things-to-know-about-ltcg-on-mutual-funds) — Bajaj Finserv AMC, 27 May 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: 12.5% LTCG above ₹1.25 lakh (aggregate per year), 20% STCG, 12-month threshold, applies under the Income-tax Act 2025; a switch-out is a redemption.
- [Taxation of Mutual Funds: What Every Investor Needs to Know](https://www.tatamutualfund.com/blogs/taxation-mutual-funds-what-every-investor-needs-know-stcg-ltcg-dividend-income-and-elss) — Tata Mutual Fund. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: debt funds bought after 31 Mar 2023 taxed at slab rates regardless of holding period.
- [What is an exit load in mutual funds](https://www.bajajamc.com/glossary/what-is-an-exit-load-in-mutual-funds) — Bajaj Finserv AMC, 4 Jan 2024. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: FIFO on redemption; each SIP instalment is a separate purchase with its own clock.
- [Disclose absolute commissions of distributors in CAS: SEBI](https://cafemutual.com/news/industry/6454-disclose-absolute-commissions-of-distributors-in-cas-sebi) — Cafemutual, 21 Sep 2016. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: CAS shows rupee commission paid per scheme and the plan's average TER for the half-year, from 1 Oct 2016.
- [There's a commission of ₹27,335 crore inside your mutual fund](https://1finance.co.in/blog/mutual-fund-distribution-commission-india/) — 1 Finance, 24 Aug 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: total distribution commission paid in a year (their analysis of AMFI disclosures).
- [SEBI updates guidelines for investment advisers (2025)](https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-updates-guidelines-investment-advisers-2025.html) — TaxGuru, on SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/003 (8 Jan 2025). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: RIA fee caps of 2.5% of AUA or ₹1,51,000 a year per family.
- [Zerodha vs Groww debate: Are direct mutual funds always better, or is advice worth paying for?](https://www.businesstoday.in/mutual-funds/story/zerodha-vs-groww-debate-are-direct-mutual-funds-always-better-or-is-advice-worth-paying-for-542346-2026-07-10) — Business Today, 10 Jul 2026. Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: Groww offering regular plans through Groww Prime; Zerodha sells direct plans only.
