## Primary / official

- [Framework for Facilitating Small Value Digital Payments in Offline Mode](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12215&Mode=0) — Reserve Bank of India, 3 Jan 2022, updated 4 Dec 2024. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: UPI Lite limits (₹1,000 per transaction, ₹5,000 total); no additional authentication; alerts not real-time; top-up online with authentication; Integrated Ombudsman.

## Reputable secondary

- [UPI MDR explained: No charges for consumers, 0.4% fee on select merchant transactions above ₹2,000](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/09/16/npci-released-upi-mdr-faqs-explained/) — SCC Online, 16 Sep 2026 (summary of NPCI's UPI MDR FAQs of 15 Sep 2026). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: MDR rates, caps, special categories, exemptions, no pass-through to consumers, no platform fees, credit-line exclusion, >95% of volume excluded.
- [UPI transaction limits for these categories set to change from September 15, 2025](https://www.caalley.com/news-updates/indian-news/upi-transaction-limits-for-these-categories-set-to-change-from-september-15-2025-see-the-list) — CAalley, Sep 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: high-value P2M category limits; banks may set lower limits.
