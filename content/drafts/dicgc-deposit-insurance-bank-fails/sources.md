# Sources: DICGC deposit insurance

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [A Guide to Deposit Insurance](https://www.dicgc.org.in/guide-to-deposit-insurance), DICGC (with FAQs expanded). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: insured banks; covered and excluded deposits; not covered (NBFC, MFs, stocks, ETFs, crypto); ₹5 lakh principal + interest in the same right and capacity; the interest example; branches aggregated; separate banks; capacities; joint-account ordering rule (w.e.f. 26 Apr 2007); set-off of dues; premium paid by the bank; compulsory; liquidation (two months to the liquidator); Section 18A timeline (45/30/15 days, 90-day cap); willingness form with no cut-off.
