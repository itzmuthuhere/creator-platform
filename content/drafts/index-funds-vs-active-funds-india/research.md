# Research notes: Index funds vs active funds in India

Internal working file. Not published.

## 1. Source videos (in my own words)

- **Primary:** Should You Choose Nifty Midcap150 Index or Active Midcap Funds? (6-Year Data) (moniwise), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KwmoMrRAHvc. ~10 months old. Compares 23 regular-plan active mid-cap funds with a Nifty Midcap 150 index fund (Sep 2019–Sep 2025): 13 of 23 beat it over one year, 8 over 3/5/6 years, only 4 consistently; 7 beat it on SIP returns; on 1-year rolling returns 9 beat it more than half the time, 5 more than 60%. Notes SEBI's 2017 category rules limit older history.
- **Second:** Active MF vs Passive MF vs Both (freefincal, M Pattabiraman), https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gHnWZn5wY1o. ~8 months old. Roughly half of active funds beat a reasonable benchmark; winners don't persist; prefers index funds over ETFs and plain market-cap indices over smart beta; Nifty 500 is still ~65% large cap; planning (goals, allocation, risk) matters more than the active/passive choice; don't act superior either way.
