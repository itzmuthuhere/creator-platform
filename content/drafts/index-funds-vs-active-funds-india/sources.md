## Primary / official

- [SPIVA India Mid-Year 2026](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/spiva/article/spiva-india/) — S&P Dow Jones Indices, 21 Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: underperformance rates for large-cap, ELSS and mid/small-cap funds over 3, 5 and 10 years and H1 2026; benchmarks used.
- [TER of MF Schemes](https://www.amfiindia.com/ter-of-mf-schemes) — AMFI, September 2026 data via the page's API (`scripts/research/amfi-ter-gap.mjs`). Accessed 2026-10-02. Used for: direct-plan TERs of plain Nifty 50 index funds and active large-cap funds.
