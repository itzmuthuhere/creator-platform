# Sources: Fixed vs floating home loan rates

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [Home Loan Interest Rates](https://homeloans.hdfc.bank.in/checklist/home-loan-interest-rates), HDFC Bank (page titled Sep 2026). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: adjustable rate = repo + 2.50% to 7.95% (7.75%–13.20%) with repo at 5.25%; TruFixed is fixed for a period such as the first two years, then adjustable.
- [FAQs: Reset of Floating Interest Rate on EMI based Personal Loans](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/Upload/English/FAQs/PDFs/FAQRFIR10012025.pdf), RBI, updated 1 Oct 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the fixed-rate switch at reset "where such an option is provided"; switching charges allowed if disclosed.
- [RBI bars pre-payment charges on floating rate loans](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/rbi-bars-pre-payment-charges-on-transfer-of-floating-rate-loans-for-individual-borrowers/), News On AIR. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the ban covers floating-rate loans to individuals; for others, charges must be disclosed in the sanction letter, agreement and KFS.

## Reputable secondary

- [Home Loan Interest Rates 2026](https://www.bankbazaar.com/home-loan-interest-rate.html), BankBazaar, Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: fixed vs floating rates for BoB, PNB, Kotak, HSBC, Axis.
- [RBI Policy October 2026](https://www.gopocket.in/news/rbi-policy-october-2026-repo-rate-inflation-emi-outlook), GoPocket, 29 Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: repo 5.25%; inflation rising in 2026.

## Source videos

- [Fixed vs Floating Interest Rate: Home Loan Ke Liye Kaunsa Hai Best?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ch0G9JdqMA), Moneyview (YouTube, Hindi). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: hybrid loans fixed for 3–5 years; the tenure trap anecdote (not used); floating prepayment flexibility; the ₹25 lakh/9%/20-year one-extra-EMI example (about 16 years).
- [Fixed or Floating, Flat or Reducing Interest rate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SwBSNfHYDWM), CA Hardik K. Thacker (YouTube, Hindi). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: fixed and floating both on reducing balance, unlike flat rates.
