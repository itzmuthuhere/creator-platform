# Sources: Home loan balance transfer

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [Conversion Fees](https://homeloans.hdfc.bank.in/conversion-fees), HDFC Bank. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 0.25% of principal outstanding or ₹5,000 plus taxes, whichever is lower, for switching a fully disbursed variable-rate housing loan to a lower rate.
- [RBI bars pre-payment charges on transfer of floating rate loans for individual borrowers](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/rbi-bars-pre-payment-charges-on-transfer-of-floating-rate-loans-for-individual-borrowers/), News On AIR (Prasar Bharati). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: Pre-payment Charges on Loans Directions, 2025; individuals, non-business, floating rate; no lock-in; full or partial; effective 2026.

- [Levy of foreclosure charges/pre-payment penalty on floating rate term loans](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/english/Scripts/Notification.aspx?Id=1381), RBI, 7 May 2014. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: banks can't charge foreclosure/pre-payment penalties on floating-rate term loans to individuals.

- [RBI Policy October 2026: Repo Rate, Inflation & EMI Outlook](https://www.gopocket.in/news/rbi-policy-october-2026-repo-rate-inflation-emi-outlook), GoPocket, 29 Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03.
