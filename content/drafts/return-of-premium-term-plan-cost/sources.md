# Sources: Return of premium term insurance

One line per source actually consulted. Only list pages you opened and read.

## Primary / official

- [Recommendations of the 56th Meeting of the GST Council](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2163555), PIB, 3 Sep 2025. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: exemption of GST on all individual life insurance policies; services rate changes effective 22 Sep 2025.
- [Term Plan with Return of Premium](https://www.bajajlifeinsurance.com/term-insurance-plans/term-plan-with-return-of-premium.html), Bajaj Life Insurance, modified 3 Jun 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: definition of the return of premium (base premiums, excluding extra premium and taxes); 80C on premiums (old regime); maturity tax-exempt subject to conditions. (Its premium illustration was internally inconsistent and wasn't used.)

## Reputable secondary

- [Term Insurance With Return of Premium – TROP Explained](https://joinditto.in/term-insurance/term-insurance-with-return-of-premium/), Ditto, updated 24 Sep 2026. Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: 2026 quotes (₹2 crore to age 65: age 25 ₹19,719 vs ₹47,075; age 30 ₹25,153 vs ₹66,841); riders not refunded; GST cut to 0% from 22 Sep 2025; surrender value lower in early years; the 6% opportunity-cost comparison.

## Source videos

- [Term Life Insurance with REFUND OPTION | Zero Cost Term Plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0kSKsG1Zb8A), Ditto Insurance (YouTube). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: zero-cost exit option; usually priced near normal term; the catch of a longer term to qualify; ROP costs 70–100% more (not used, as the 2026 quotes are higher). Its GST-not-refunded point is outdated (GST exempt since Sep 2025).
- [Term Insurance With or Without RETURN OF PREMIUM? With Excel Calculation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1-x5wIc-D5o), Gurleen Kaur Tikku (YouTube, Hindi). Accessed 2026-10-03. Used for: the invest-the-difference conclusion (poor auto-captions; general point only).
