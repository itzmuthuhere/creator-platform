# DigiLocker: how to set it up, and where its documents count as originals

DigiLocker is the government's document wallet: an app and website where your driving licence, vehicle RC, Aadhaar, PAN, school mark sheets and many state certificates can be pulled directly from the departments that issued them. Those copies count as valid documents in many situations, including a traffic check and a university admission. But one detail decides whether a DigiLocker document is accepted: whether it's an **issued** document or one you **uploaded** yourself.

This guide explains that difference, sets out how to create an account and fetch your documents, and lists where DigiLocker documents are formally accepted, and where you'll still need the original.

## Issued vs uploaded: the difference that matters

DigiLocker has two places for documents, as its [FAQ](https://www.digilocker.gov.in/web/about/faq) explains:

| | Issued documents | Uploaded documents (DigiLocker Drive) |
|---|---|---|
| Where they come from | Fetched live from the issuing department's records | A scan or photo you added yourself |
| Can they be verified? | Yes: digitally signed, often with a QR code | No more than any photocopy |
| Treated as original? | Yes, in the cases below | No |
| Examples | Driving licence, RC, Aadhaar, PAN verification record, CBSE mark sheets | Anything you scan: a rent agreement, an old certificate |

The FAQ describes issued documents as "legally at par with originals". That's the part that matters. A photo of your licence uploaded to the Drive is just a photo. The licence fetched into the Issued section is the record itself, signed by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways.

Some videos skip this. Tech With Smile's [walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2rP3K1u1Rvc), for example, says every ID in DigiLocker is valid; strictly, that applies to the issued ones.

## Setting up an account

1. Install the DigiLocker app or open digilocker.gov.in. Only an Indian mobile number works.
2. Sign up with your mobile number or Aadhaar number. You'll get an OTP, valid for 10 minutes.
3. Set a six-digit **security PIN**. You'll use it with the OTP each time you log in.
4. Link your Aadhaar if you didn't sign up with it. You'll need the mobile number registered with your Aadhaar for this OTP. Once it's linked, your digital Aadhaar, which DigiLocker says is the same as the e-Aadhaar from UIDAI, appears in Issued documents.

Your name and date of birth in DigiLocker come from Aadhaar and can't be edited separately. If they're wrong, fix them in Aadhaar first.

PIR Nexus Point's [Tamil explainer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xNanOWg49-E) also shows how to add family members as dependent profiles under one mobile number, which helps if you manage your parents' or children's documents.

## Getting your documents

Use **Search** or **Browse**, pick the document, and enter the details the issuer needs to find your record:

| Document | What you enter |
|---|---|
| Driving licence | Licence number; your Aadhaar name must match the licence record |
| Vehicle RC | Registration number and chassis/engine details as asked |
| PAN verification record | Your PAN details, after linking Aadhaar |
| CBSE mark sheets and certificates | Year and roll number |
| State certificates (income, caste, community, etc.) | Certificate or application number; available issuers vary by state |
| Some electricity bills | Consumer or account number |

Once fetched, the document is saved as a link in Issued documents; DigiLocker pulls it from the issuer in real time when you open it.

**CBSE students** usually already have an account: DigiLocker creates one using the mobile number given to CBSE, and the first security PIN is the last six digits of the roll number.

## Where issued documents are accepted

These are the formal ones, each backed by an order or circular:

- **Traffic checks (driving licence and RC).** The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways recognised DigiLocker for digital DL and RC in a notification dated 8 August 2018, and wrote to all states that a DL or RC shown from DigiLocker's Issued section is legally at par with the original under the IT Act, according to DigiLocker's FAQ. An officer can check it by scanning its QR code or verifying MoRTH's digital signature.
- **Universities and colleges.** In January 2022, UGC asked all institutions to [accept degrees and mark sheets](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/headlines/1874405-ugc-requests-all-academic-institutions-to-accept-degree-mark-sheets-through-digilocker-platform) in DigiLocker's issued documents as valid documents.
- **Insurance.** IRDAI's [February 2021 circular](https://www.teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/12674/irdai-has-issued-a-circular-for-the-issuance-of-digital-insurance-poli/) asked insurers to connect their systems to DigiLocker so policyholders can keep policies there.
- **Passports.** Since December 2025, your [Passport Verification Record](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/meity-mea-launch-paperless-passport-verification-via-digilocker/) can be stored and shared through DigiLocker's Issued section.
- **Identity and address proof generally.** Many government services and regulated businesses can request documents from your DigiLocker with your consent, instead of asking for photocopies. Every share is logged in your account's activity.

## When you'll still need the original

- **Where the person checking doesn't know the rules.** A traffic officer is covered by MoRTH's instruction, but a private office, landlord or shop may still ask for the physical document. They can choose what they accept.
- **Uploaded documents.** Anything in your Drive is only a copy.
- **Documents your issuer hasn't connected.** If your university, board or state department isn't a DigiLocker issuer, there's nothing to fetch; the original stays the official version.
- **Your phone is dead.** Carrying the physical licence while driving is still a sensible backup.

## Keeping your account safe

DigiLocker uses an OTP and your PIN at every login, and shares documents only with your consent. Your part:

- Don't share your PIN or OTP with anyone who calls about "DigiLocker verification".
- Use a screen lock on your phone; the app holds identity documents.
- Check the **Activity** section occasionally for shares you don't recognise.

Two-step login is what protects the account; our guide to [how two-factor authentication stops account hacks](https://techpulzo.in/how-two-factor-authentication-actually-stops-account-hacks) explains why.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Details did not match the issuer data":** a typo in the number, or your name differs between Aadhaar and the issuer's record. Check spelling against the original document.
- **"No record available in issuer database":** for a DL or RC, the record may not be in the national register yet. Check its status on Parivahan; an old licence may need to be updated at the RTO before it appears.
- **"Mobile number is not linked with Aadhaar":** the Aadhaar OTP goes to the number registered with Aadhaar, which may be an old one. Update it with UIDAI first.
- **"Aadhaar already registered":** you probably have an older DigiLocker account. Log in with your Aadhaar number instead of creating a new one.

If you've lost a PAN card rather than just needing a copy, our guide to [getting a duplicate PAN card](https://techpulzo.in/how-to-get-a-duplicate-pan-card-online) covers the replacement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a driving licence in DigiLocker valid during a traffic check?

Yes, if it's in the Issued documents section. MoRTH has told all states that a driving licence or RC shown from DigiLocker's Issued section is legally equal to the original under the IT Act. A scanned photo uploaded to your Drive doesn't count.

### What is the difference between issued and uploaded documents in DigiLocker?

Issued documents are fetched directly from the department that issued them and are digitally signed, so they can be verified. Uploaded documents are scans you add yourself; they're stored for convenience but carry no more weight than a photocopy.

### Can I use DigiLocker without Aadhaar?

You can sign up with just a mobile number, but most issued documents, including your driving licence and PAN record, need your Aadhaar linked so DigiLocker can match your identity with the issuer's record.

### Are DigiLocker mark sheets accepted for college admission?

UGC asked universities and colleges in January 2022 to accept degrees and mark sheets from DigiLocker's issued documents as valid. CBSE and many state boards and universities issue them there. Check that the document appears in your Issued section, not as an upload.

### Is DigiLocker safe?

It requires an OTP and a security PIN to log in, shares documents only with your consent, and logs each share in your account. The main risks are on your side: sharing your OTP or PIN, or leaving your phone unlocked.

## Set it up before you need it

Create the account, link Aadhaar, and fetch your driving licence, RC, PAN and mark sheets now, while nothing is urgent. Then the day you forget your wallet or a college asks for certificates, they're already there in the form that counts.
