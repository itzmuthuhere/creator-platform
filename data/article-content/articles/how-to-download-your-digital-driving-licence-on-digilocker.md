A driving licence pulled into DigiLocker comes straight from the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways database, digitally signed by the issuing authority. Under the IT Act, that signature makes it legally equivalent to the physical card during a traffic check — which is why it's worth setting up before you ever need it.

## Set up DigiLocker if you haven't already
Download the DigiLocker app or go to digilocker.gov.in. Sign up using your mobile number — you'll get an OTP, then you set a 6-digit PIN or use your Aadhaar for verification. If you link Aadhaar during setup, DigiLocker can auto-fetch documents issued against that Aadhaar-linked mobile number, which speeds up the next step considerably.

## Pull the driving licence into your account
Inside the app, go to the Search Documents or Issued Documents section and look for Transport under the department list, or search directly for 'Driving Licence'. Select the issuer as your state's Transport Department. You'll be asked for your DL number and date of birth exactly as they appear on your physical licence — even a small formatting mismatch, like a missing leading zero, can cause the pull to fail.

## Verify and save it
Once fetched, the document appears under Issued Documents with a green verified tick, meaning it's digitally signed by the transport authority and not just an uploaded scan. Tap it to view details, and you can generate a shareable PDF with a QR code from the same screen for offline use, like showing it during a vehicle insurance claim.

## What to do if the fetch fails
The most common failure is a records mismatch — your DL number exists in the state RTO database but the date of birth or name spelling doesn't match what DigiLocker is checking against. In that case, go to your state's Sarathi or Parivahan portal, verify what's on record there, and get any correction filed at the RTO before trying the DigiLocker pull again. A second common issue is simply that your state hasn't fully digitized older licence records yet, in which case you may need to apply for a renewed physical card first.

## Adding Family Members' Documents to the Same Account
DigiLocker's Family Member feature lets you link and access documents for dependents, useful for a parent managing an elderly family member's documents or for accessing a spouse's DL and RC without needing separate logins. Each family member needs to individually consent to the linking through their own OTP verification before their documents become visible in your account, though.

## What Happens If Your State Hasn't Digitized Your Specific Licence Yet
Digitization coverage varies significantly by state and by how old the original licence issuance was. Older licences issued before a state's RTO fully digitized its records sometimes simply aren't available for DigiLocker pull yet, even though the physical card is completely valid. In that case, the physical card remains your only option until the state completes that backlog, and there's no user-side fix beyond periodically retrying the pull.

## Keeping the App Actually Usable During a Real Stop
A DigiLocker document is only useful if the app is accessible in the moment. Keeping the app logged in with a quick-unlock PIN, instead of requiring a fresh OTP login every time, avoids a scenario where poor network coverage during a roadside stop prevents you from receiving the OTP needed to log back in.

## DigiLocker Documents and Insurance Claim Processing
Beyond traffic stops, a digitally signed DL and RC pulled from DigiLocker are increasingly accepted directly by insurance companies during claim processing, since the digital signature already confirms authenticity in a way a photographed physical document doesn't, which can shave real time off a claims process that often gets held up on document verification specifically.

## Why It's Worth Doing This Even If You Rarely Drive
Even for occasional drivers, having the DL ready in DigiLocker before it's needed removes one more thing to scramble for during a rental car pickup, a sudden road trip, or lending a vehicle to someone who needs to verify your licence details remotely — situations where digging out a physical card from a drawer at home isn't an option.

## Does it hold up if a traffic cop stops you?
Yes — under the amended Motor Vehicles Act framework, producing documents via DigiLocker or the mParivahan app is accepted as valid production of the physical document, and police aren't supposed to seize your phone or insist on the paper original when you show it this way. Keep the app updated and your PIN handy, since a locked or logged-out app defeats the purpose during an actual stop.

## mParivahan as a Second Option
Alongside DigiLocker, the mParivahan app, also run by the Ministry of Road Transport, pulls the same underlying RTO data and offers a similar digital licence and RC display. The two apps draw from the same government database, so there's no real accuracy difference between them; mParivahan simply bundles a few extra vehicle-focused tools like challan status and fitness certificate checks alongside the document display, which some drivers find more convenient to keep in one place.

## Using It for Rental Cars and Shared Mobility Apps
Self-drive rental services and car-sharing apps increasingly accept a DigiLocker-verified licence during pickup verification, particularly for app-based bookings where an agent checks your documents on a phone screen rather than a physical counter. Having it ready before arriving at pickup avoids a scenario where a slow mobile network delays the OTP login right when you need document access fastest.

## If Your RTO Record Itself Has an Error
Sometimes the DigiLocker pull fails not because of a formatting mismatch on your end but because the underlying RTO record has a genuine data entry error, a misspelled name or wrong date of birth in the government database itself. This requires a correction request filed directly at the issuing RTO, with your original licence and ID proof, rather than anything fixable from within the DigiLocker app. It's a slower process, typically a few weeks, so it's worth checking well before you need the digital copy for something time-sensitive.

## Adding a Learner's Licence Before It Converts
A learner's licence can also be pulled into DigiLocker the same way, useful during the learning period before it converts to a full licence. Once the full licence is issued after the mandatory waiting period, the learner's licence entry in DigiLocker doesn't automatically update, so you'll need to search and add the new permanent licence separately once it's been issued.

## A Note on Privacy and Access
DigiLocker documents are only accessible after your own PIN or biometric unlock, and the app doesn't share your documents with any third party without your explicit consent through its share flow. Showing a document to a traffic officer through the app's own display, rather than handing over your unlocked phone, keeps you in control of what else on the device is visible during that interaction.

## What to Do If You're Traveling to a State With Poor Connectivity
If you know you're headed somewhere with unreliable mobile data, a hill route or a rural stretch, generating and saving the shareable PDF with its QR code in advance, while you still have a strong connection, means you can show it offline from your phone's file storage even if the DigiLocker app itself can't load without data at the actual moment you're stopped.

## Renewing a Licence Doesn't Automatically Update the DigiLocker Copy
When a licence gets renewed at the RTO, whether for expiry or address change, the DigiLocker-linked copy doesn't refresh itself. You need to redo the pull-in step after the physical renewal is processed and reflected in the RTO database, typically a few days after the renewal, otherwise DigiLocker keeps showing the old, technically expired version even though your physical card is current.

## Keeping a Backup Outside the App Too
Beyond the app itself, saving a copy of the generated PDF to your phone's regular file storage or emailing it to yourself gives a fallback if the DigiLocker app has an outage or update issue at a moment you need the document, which does happen occasionally on any government-run digital service and is worth planning around rather than assuming perfect uptime.

## What This Actually Saves You Long Term
Once set up, this whole process takes about ten minutes total and never needs repeating except after a renewal. Compared to the recurring hassle of a misplaced physical card, a laminated copy that's still technically not valid for official checks, or a scramble before a road trip, it's one of the more clearly worthwhile ten-minute tasks in the entire category of digital paperwork, and one that keeps paying off every single time you need the document afterward without any extra effort.
It's worth doing the same for your RC (registration certificate) and insurance while you're at it, so every document a roadside stop could ask for lives in one place. With your vehicle documents and DL both sitting in DigiLocker, there's no real need to carry the physical folder in your car anymore.
